## transcription-note

Digital OCR (ABBYY FineReader 8.0), as served by the Internet Archive, of a University of California copy digitised by Google; not verified against print. The OCR misreads many letters, accents, apostrophes and some figures (among them the '26' of footnote 2 on p. 678, which it reads '86'), and runs the footnotes, set in smaller type, into the text. Whitespace collapsed; all other characters retained, including OCR errors, split words, hyphenation marks, running heads with page numbers, footnotes and the scanner's marks interleaved with the text. Local headings are editorial. Eight bounded passages: the documents of 10 October 1806 on Saalfeld, from the Emperor's letter to the grand-duc de Berg (p. 456) to General Milhaud's report to the grand-duc de Berg from Pösneck (p. 476), with M. de Mumpfling's report printed in the footnotes; documents of 11 October 1806 on Saalfeld, from Marshal Lannes's letter to the Emperor from Saalfeld (p. 494) to the end of General Foucher's report to General Songis on the 5th corps's artillery (p. 497), with the surgeon's report on Prince Louis's wounds and Marshal Lannes's letter to the major general from Neustadt between them; the 2nd Bulletin of the Grande Armée, from its heading (p. 522) to its last sentence (p. 525); the situation of the Prussian army from the 32nd Bulletin, with Foucart's estimates and his extract of Lieutenant-Colonel von der Goltz (pp. 611-613); the 5th Bulletin of the Grande Armée with Foucart's notes (pp. 614-620); General Suchet's report to Marshal Lannes on Jena, from its heading to its loss total, with Foucart's notes (pp. 632-635); Foucart's discussion of the orders to Marshal Davout and of the forces at Jena, with an extract of the 3rd corps's journal of operations and his tables (pp. 668-671); and, on Auerstedt, Marshal Davout's report and the 3rd corps's journal of operations with Foucart's notes, to the losses (pp. 672-684).
