# Foucart, Campagne de Prusse (1806): Iéna (1887): p.635 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.635 from the registered page image foucart-prusse-1887-t1-p635-image-v1. The two paragraphs from 'L'artillerie a tiré' to '75 officiers.' are transcribed, as printed; words split by a line-end hyphen are rejoined, a hyphen that belongs to a compound is kept ('avant-garde', 'général-major', 'très-considérable'), line breaks are not kept, italics are not marked, superscript letters are written inline ('1re', '10e') and small capitals as capitals, a note-reference figure is written as a superscript figure ('¹') directly after the word or punctuation it follows and a footnote is given after the main text as its own paragraph beginning with its figure ('1.'), guillemets are written « » with a space inside them and the guillemets the print repeats at the start of each line of a quotation are not kept, word spaces the print narrows or omits are written as normal spaces, a space the print sets before a comma is not kept, and a colon is written with a space before it and a semicolon or question mark without one, whatever space the print shows; other text is omitted. The paragraphs end General Suchet's report to Marshal Lannes, which begins on p. 632 and is followed on this page by General Foucher's report to General Songis. The figures were checked on the Internet Archive's full-size page image (3100 x 5017 pixels): the worn first figure of '75' has the slanting stem of the page's '7' (as in '70 toises') with a faint top bar, and not the flag and foot of its '1' (the OCR reads '(5'). Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR.

## p635

L'artillerie a tiré à Saalfeld et à la bataille d'Iéna 1,322 coups de canon. Elle a démonté à l'ennemi 9 pièces dont 3 ont été enlevées par les canonniers. Elle a eu 3 pièces démontées et 15 chevaux de troupe tués...

La perte totale en tués ou blessés s'élève à 2,645 parmi lesquels 75 officiers.
