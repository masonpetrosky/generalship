## rawlins-1864-01-29

No. 1. Reports of Brig . Gen. John A. Raivlins, U. 8. Army , Chief of Staff, Division of the Mississippi. Nashville, Tenn., January 29, 1864. On the morning of the 25th [26tli] the enemy, 600 strong, attacked our garrison, of about 100, at Athens. After a two-hours’ fight the enemy was repulsed and driven. Our loss 20; the enemy’s much greater. On the 27th [25th] Colonel Miller had a severe fight on this side of Florence, repulsing the enemy. Our loss 15 killed, 25 wounded. JNO. A. RAWLINS, Brigadier- General and Chief of Staff. Col. J. C. Kelton.
