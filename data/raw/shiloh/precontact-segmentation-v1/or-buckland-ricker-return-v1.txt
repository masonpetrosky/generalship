# Ralph P. Buckland and Elbridge G. Ricker: selected precontact passages, OR I.X.1

## transcription-note

Selected passages only, not complete reports. Whitespace and line-wrap hyphens normalized. Headings and signatures are inspected in the retained page images; omissions between sections are intentional. Source statements and clocks remain attributed and unadjudicated.

## p91-buckland

The cavalry pursued, and we followed until it was ascertained that the enemy were in force a short distance ahead, when we returned, in company with the cavalry.

## p92-ricker-retirement

Colonel Buckland coming up at this time with his command, we formed and retired in good order, bringing off 9 prisoners.

## p92-ricker-framing

I acknowledge God's mercy in protecting our men under the terrible fire poured upon us by the enemy in the opening fight of the great battle of Pittsburg.
