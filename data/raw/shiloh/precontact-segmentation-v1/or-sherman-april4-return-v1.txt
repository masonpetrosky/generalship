## p89-heading

HEADQUARTERS FIFTH DIVISION, Camp Shiloh, Tenn., April 5, 1862.

## p90-return

As soon as I heard artillery I advanced with two regiments of infantry and took position and remained until the scattered companies of infantry and cavalry returned. This was after night.
