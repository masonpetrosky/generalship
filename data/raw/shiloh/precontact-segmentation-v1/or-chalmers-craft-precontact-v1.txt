# James R. Chalmers and Henry Craft: selected precontact passages, OR I.X.2

## transcription-note

Selected passages only, not complete reports. Whitespace and line-wrap hyphens normalized. Headings and signatures are inspected in the retained page images; omissions between sections are intentional. Source statements and clocks remain attributed and unadjudicated.

## p387-provisional

Colonel Clanton has just reported verbally to me that the enemy's pickets attacked his advance pickets about daylight this morning, and he fears that two of his men were captured, though he was not able to state accurately the facts. As soon as I am accurately informed I will report to you.

## p388-update

General Chalmers directs me to inform you that information has just come in that the enemy, supposed to be one regiment, have driven in our pickets on both the Pittsburg and Savannah roads. The general has advanced two regiments up each of these roads, which are now on the march. He has gone himself to direct the advance.
