# S. A. M. Wood: selected precontact passages, OR I.X.1

## transcription-note

Selected passages only, not complete reports. Whitespace and line-wrap hyphens normalized. Headings and signatures are inspected in the retained page images; omissions between sections are intentional. Source statements and clocks remain attributed and unadjudicated.

## p590-heading

Near Corinth, Miss., April 15, 1862.

## p591-saturday

No advance occurred on Saturday. Major Hardcastle's battalion was thrown out 400 yards in advance, on picket duty, during the night; Colonel McKoin in rear as reserve.

## p591-sunday

We slept on our arms in line of battle on Saturday night. As early as 5 o'clock Sunday morning firing occurred between our pickets and the enemy. I sent Captain Clare, of my staff, to instruct Major Hardcastle to hold his position until the brigade came up. The order was faithfully carried out and executed.
