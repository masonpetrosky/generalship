# Worthington composite: appended Facts Developed, p.2 items 11 and 14

## transcription-note

Selected passages, not a full transcription. Omitted intervening text is not joined. Whitespace, line-wrap hyphens and typographic quotes/apostrophes normalized; small capitals represented as ordinary capitals. No lexical modernization. Publication and composition dates of the appended Facts Developed tract are unknown. Its heading dates the court-martial August 1862, not publication. The composite front title is undated; a different bound work, Flank March, has an 1880 imprint. Do not apply a catalog date for Brief History to this entire composite. "It was proven" is Worthington's assertion, not this project's finding; no official trial transcript is inspected.

## p2-item11

11th. It was proven that a picket post three-quarters of a mile from his centre was all day occupied by the enemy, and with artillery in the afternoon, which he repeatedly swears was not the fact (on the 5th).

## p2-item14

14th. It was proven that the pickets of the 46th Ohio, driven back on the 5th at 7 A. M., were but half a mile from the camp the balance of the day.
