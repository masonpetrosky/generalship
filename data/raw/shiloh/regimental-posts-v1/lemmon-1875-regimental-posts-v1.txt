# Lemmon reunion speech: selected April 4 narrative and April 6 transition, pp.5–7

## transcription-note

Selected passages, not a full transcription. Omitted intervening text is not joined. Whitespace, line-wrap hyphens and typographic quotes/apostrophes normalized; small capitals represented as ordinary capitals. No lexical modernization. The printed heading gives speech delivery at Fremont, Ohio, June 17, 1875. Delivery date is not an independently verified composition or printing day; those are null. The inspected pp.5–7 move from April 4 to April 6–7 without a Saturday Howell narrative. This bounded omission is not evidence of quiet; the rain interruption is in the April 4 narrative.

## p5-6-april4

On April 4th, 1862, about 2 P. M., a considerable force of rebel cavalry attacked the left of the picket line in front of Buckland's brigade, and captured a Lieutenant and seven men of the 70th Ohio Regiment.

## p6-rescuers

Upon his arrival Gen. Buckland took Cos. A, D and I, of his regiment, and started into the woods in the direction of the firing, directing Col. Cockerill to come to his aid if the firing should become heavier.

## p6-rain

A violent rain storm set in which for a time stopped all firing; as soon as the rain slackened the firing was resumed,

## p7-transition

On April 6th and 7th came Shiloh with its carnage, its thirty-six hours of constant strain, excitement, hopes and fears.
