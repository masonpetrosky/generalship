## O-deployment-and-contact

This corps left Corinth April 3d, by the Ridge and Bark roads and deployed in line of battle on this ground about noon Saturday, April 5, 1862; the 1st Brigade on the right of the Pittsburg Road; the 2d and 3d Brigades to the left of said road. The picket in front of this Corps was attacked at 4.55 a.m. April 6, 1862 and at 6.30 the Corps advanced to the attack of the Union camps.
