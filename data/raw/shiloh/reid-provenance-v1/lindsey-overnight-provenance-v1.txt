## p18-overnight

On Saturday, April 5, Companies B and K were on picket. During the night the enemy was feeling the lines constantly, and at daylight his column could be seen deploying in the distance. At sunrise a Confederate cavalry officer emerged from the woods within thirty yards of the picket line and checked his horse. He stood for a moment in seeming composure, and then inquired, "Are these Union pickets?" He was told they were, and was ordered to come up.
