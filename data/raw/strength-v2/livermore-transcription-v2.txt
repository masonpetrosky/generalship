# Livermore, Numbers and Losses (1901): transcription of selected battle entries, v2

## transcription-note

Manual transcription from the registered page images livermore-p{N}-image-v1 (Internet Archive numberslosses00liverich). This v2 extends livermore-transcription-v1 with further pages: every v1 page section is reproduced unchanged, and sections are added for printed pages that carry an entry matching a cohort-v2 decisive, non-aggregate engagement. A section named p{N}-supplement transcribes such an entry on a printed page whose v1 section is reproduced unchanged and does not include it. Entry lines and the numbered notes that bear on how a figure was formed are transcribed; loss lines and some notes are omitted, except that a loss line is kept where a transcribed note is attached to it. Leader dots are omitted. Superscript note numbers and source letters are shown in square brackets, for example [1] and [a]. Fractions printed as small figures are written with a slash. In the sections added in v2, a word divided at a line end is rejoined, and figures and fractions are given as printed even where the arithmetic does not agree. Nothing else is normalized. Not checked against another copy of the print.
