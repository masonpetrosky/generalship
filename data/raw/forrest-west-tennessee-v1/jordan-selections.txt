We are aware of the belief obtaining among the Southern people that the time has not yet come for the publication of histories or memoirs of the more prominent operations of Confederate com- manders and officers. £Many, indeed, of our countrymen we know earnestly deprecate all such publications at this juncture, as fraught with the provocation of unfriendly controversies at the South ; as sure to breed ill-feeling and strife there, among the survivors of the battles, marches, and exploits recounted^ We are conscious, more- over, how impossible it were so to write the annals we here present as to steer clear of giving pain to some, umbrage to others, dissat- isfaction also, and some grounds for disputation and criticism.
