# Koch, Mémoires de Masséna, t. V (1850): p.419 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.419 from the registered page image koch-massena-t5-p419-image-v1. The heading of pièce justificative n° IX, the list of generals, the headings of the six sections of the table with their bracketed section totals, the single row of the last section, the total and the N. B. are transcribed, as printed, one per line; the unit rows of the first five sections are not transcribed; words split by a line-end hyphen are rejoined, a hyphen that belongs to a compound is kept ('avant-garde', 'lieutenant-général', 'dit-on'), line breaks are not kept, italics are not marked, superscript letters are written inline ('1er', '42e') and small capitals as capitals, word spaces the print narrows or omits are written as normal spaces, and a colon is written with a space before it and a semicolon or question mark without one, whatever space the print shows; other text is omitted. The generals printed beside a brace are given one per line, and the words printed beside the brace on the line after them; each section's total, printed beside the brace that covers its rows, is given on the line '[brace total]', with the column separated by ' | '. Leader dots are omitted. 'GAETE' is printed without the diaeresis, and 'LANCHANTIN' is printed so. The unit rows are not transcribed because several of their regiment and company numbers are heavily inked. The table was read on crops of the full-size scan (n429), which is not registered. Each section total equals the sum of the figures of the rows it brackets as read on the crops (Artillerie 488, 95, 43, 85 and 36; Génie 27 and 211; Infanterie 300, 506, 1,274, 1,410, 1,555, 1,350, 156, 738 and 547; Cavalerie 82, 120, 10, 23, 265 and 272; Infanterie étrangère 803, 533, 531 and 87), and the printed total, 12,016, equals the sum of the six section totals; the last figures of '747' and '238' and of the row figure 1,350 are heavily inked or damaged and are read by their shapes and these sums. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR.
