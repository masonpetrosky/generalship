## p393

I had succeeded, by sheer physical force, in bringing the guns of position and the field-guns to the last echelon;⁶ and had made some redoubts on the Campo Tenese.⁷ Here I intended to place my recruits, supposing the enemy were successful in all the first encounters and it should become necessary to hold this position for a day or two, while the two wings were effecting a junction on the banks of the Coscile or the Crati. It was there that the greater part of the army-corps was to assemble.
