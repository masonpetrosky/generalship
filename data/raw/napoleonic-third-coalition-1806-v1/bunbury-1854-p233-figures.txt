During the months of May and June 1806 the intelligence which we received from all parts of Calabria led us to the belief that the French had acted rashly in pushing their troops so far to the southward; and that while they appeared to be threatening Sicily from Reggio and the neighbouring coast, they were really weak in numbers and exposed in their position. Their line of communication with Naples was of a dangerous extent; running through mountainous defiles, and across the beds of torrents: the bridges few, the roads very bad.
