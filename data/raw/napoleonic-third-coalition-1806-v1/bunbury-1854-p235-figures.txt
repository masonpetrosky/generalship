## p235

But weighing the question on both sides, we came to believe that if we could make good our landing with 5000 men in the Gulf of St. Eufemia, Reynier would find it necessary to retreat to Catanzaro; that the insurrection of both the provinces would then become general, and that Massena would be obliged to abandon the siege of Gaeta.

Our troops were at that time cantoned within narrow bounds,—most of them in Messina and Milazzo; the rest in the neighbouring villages. They were ready to step on board the transports, and as the vessels were fully victualled, there was no necessity for any preparation as to provisions.
