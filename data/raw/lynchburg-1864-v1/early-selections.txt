## early-1864-06-19

New London, June 19, 1861—9.30 a. m. General : Last evening the enemy assaulted my lines in front of Lynchburg and was repulsed by the part of my command which was up. On the arrival of the rest of the command I made arrange¬ ments to attack this morning at light, but it was discovered that the enemy were retreating, and I am now pursuing. The enemy is re¬ treating in confusion, and if the cavalry does its duty we will de¬ stroy him. J. A. EAKLY, Lieutenant- General. General R. E. Lee (Care of General Bragg).
