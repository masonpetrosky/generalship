# Livermore, Numbers and Losses (1901): transcription of selected battle entries, v1

## transcription-note

Manual transcription from the registered page images livermore-p{N}-image-v1 (Internet Archive numberslosses00liverich). Entry lines and the numbered notes that bear on how a figure was formed are transcribed; loss lines and some notes are omitted. Leader dots are omitted. Superscript note numbers and source letters are shown in square brackets, for example [1] and [a]. Fractions printed as small figures are written with a slash. Nothing else is normalized. Not checked against another copy of the print.

## p78

FORT DONELSON, FEBRUARY 12-16, 1862. UNION ARMY. In the lines and guarding the road to the left 27,000 [a] CONFEDERATE ARMY. Engaged [4] about [5] 21,000 [c] [a] 1 Grant's Memoirs, 315. [c] 1 Grant's Memoirs, 315. [4] General Pillow reported that he had "only about 13,000 troops all told" (7 W. R., 283), but 16,623 were killed, wounded, and captured, and at least 2000 more escaped (7 W. R., 275, 295). [5] There are no data for determining whether these are the numbers "present for duty," or "present for duty equipped," or "effective."
