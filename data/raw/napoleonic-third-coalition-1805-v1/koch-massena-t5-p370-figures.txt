# Koch, Mémoires de Masséna, t. V (1850): p.370 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.370 from the registered page image koch-massena-t5-p370-image-v1: the recapitulation, the men not included and the matériel that close pièce justificative n° VI, the order of battle of the Army of Italy at the time of 18 October 1805. Everything on the page is transcribed, as printed, except the running head and page number, one line per row or printed line, with the columns separated by ' | ' and the column header of the recapitulation as one line. '»' is the printed blank mark, leader dots are omitted, and the headings printed in italics are not marked. The six garrisons, printed beside a brace with one total, are given one per line, the total on the line '[brace total]'; the labels printed beside the braces of the matériel are given on their own lines before their items, and the word 'pour' between the two braces of the administration on its own line. The heading 'Récapitulation.' and several figures of the recapitulation are heavily inked: the heading is read by its letter shapes, and the figures 27, 13, 29, 8 and 59, the figure 6,812 and the third figure of 4,559 by their shapes, each agreeing with the division totals printed on pp. 368-369 and with the column sums. The Réserve row carries the 15 squadrons of Mermet's cavalry division with the men of Partonneau's division only, and the Cavalerie row the men of Mermet's, Espagne's and Pully's divisions; both are as printed. The printed totals equal the sums of their rows (41,355 men; 5,733 in the garrisons; 65,235 in the effectif), and the 54 guns of the field equipment equal the sum of the divisions' guns on pp. 368-369. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR.
