# Alombert and Colin, La campagne de 1805 en Allemagne, t. II: p.161 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.161 from the registered page image alombert-colin-t2-p161-image-v1: the return of the 4th corps at the Rhine crossing. Every row of the return is transcribed as printed, one row per line, with the columns separated by ' | ': unit; number of battalions (I), squadrons (C) or companies (A and G); men; troop horses; train horses. '»' is the printed blank mark. A label printed beside a brace that groups rows is given on its own line before those rows; '[subtotal]' marks an unlabelled printed subtotal row. Leader dots are omitted, and 'id.' is written with its period. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR. The light cavalry and artillery subtotals equal the sums of their rows, the printed total of men equals the sum of the three printed subtotals, and the printed effectif of men equals the total present plus those behind and in hospital. Two printed figures do not agree with the rows read above them: the infantry rows as read sum to 26,082, 995 more than the printed infantry subtotal of 25,087, which the printed total of men carries; and the printed total of troop horses (1,380) is 100 more than the sum of the two subtotals (1,280), which the summary's 1,946 horses (1,280 plus the 666 train horses) follows. All are transcribed as read. The printed fraction 1/2 is written '½', the abbreviation 'chx' is written inline, and the thousands separators of the summary's horse figures, unclear on the image, are written as commas. The page's two footnotes are transcribed.
