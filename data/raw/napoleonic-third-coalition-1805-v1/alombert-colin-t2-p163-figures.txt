# Alombert and Colin, La campagne de 1805 en Allemagne, t. II: p.163 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.163 from the registered page image alombert-colin-t2-p163-image-v1: the return of the 6th corps at the Rhine crossing. Every row of the return is transcribed as printed, one row per line, with the columns separated by ' | ': unit; number of battalions (I), squadrons (C) or companies (A and G); men; troop horses; train horses. '»' is the printed blank mark. A label printed beside a brace that groups rows is given on its own line before those rows; '[subtotal]' marks an unlabelled printed subtotal row. Leader dots are omitted, and 'id.' is written with its period. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR. Each printed subtotal and total equals the sum of the rows read above it, and the printed effectif equals the total present plus those behind and in hospital; the last digit of the hospital figure is faint and is read as 8, which that sum requires.
