# Alombert and Colin, La campagne de 1805 en Allemagne, t. II: p.165 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.165 from the registered page image alombert-colin-t2-p165-image-v1: the return of the cavalry reserve at the Rhine crossing, first page. Every row of the return is transcribed as printed, one row per line, with the columns separated by ' | ': unit; number of battalions (I), squadrons (C) or companies (A and G); men; troop horses; train horses. '»' is the printed blank mark. A label printed beside a brace that groups rows is given on its own line before those rows; '[subtotal]' marks an unlabelled printed subtotal row. Leader dots are omitted, and 'id.' is written with its period. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR. The men's figures sum to the printed subtotal of 14,443. The horse figures as read sum to 13,338, five fewer than the printed 13,343, which p. 166 carries into the reserve's total, so one horse figure is misread or misprinted; the middle digit of the 11e cuirassiers' horses (read 429) is faint. The first regiment of the 2e division de dragons reads '16e' on the image, as does a regiment of the 3e division; it is transcribed as read.
