# Koch, Mémoires de Masséna, t. V (1850): p.369 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.369 from the registered page image koch-massena-t5-p369-image-v1: the second part of the table of pièce justificative n° VI, the order of battle of the Army of Italy at the time of 18 October 1805 (Centre, Aile gauche, Réserve and Cavalerie). Everything on the page is transcribed, as printed, except the running head and page number and the signature 'TOM. V. 24'. The table is transcribed row by row, as printed, one row per line, with the columns separated by ' | ' (regiment; number of battalions; number of squadrons), and its two-level header as one line. '»' is the printed blank mark and 'id.' is kept. The generals of division and of brigade, printed beside braces, are given on their own lines before their rows, and '[without brigade]' marks a division's rows that no brigade brace covers; the label of a wing or arm, printed beside the brace that covers its divisions ('Centre.', 'Aile gauche.', 'Réserve.', 'Cavalerie.'), is given on its own line before them. Leader dots, and the period that runs into them after a general's name or 'Total', are omitted. Small capitals are written as capitals, superscript letters inline, and line-end hyphens rejoined. In the header the damaged last letters of 'bataillons.' are read by the header of p. 368. Each division's printed totals of battalions and squadrons equal the sums of its rows. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR.
