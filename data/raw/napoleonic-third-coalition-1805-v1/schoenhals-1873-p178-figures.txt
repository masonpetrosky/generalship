## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.178 from the registered page image schoenhals-1873-p178-image-v1. The recapitulation at the foot of the table, from 'Recapitulation.' to its sum ('Summa'), and the note ('Anmerkung') below it are transcribed, as printed; the headings are given as their own lines and their letter-spacing is not kept; each row of the recapitulation is written on one line, its label and its figures separated by ' | ' in the order of the page's figure columns, 'Bataillons', 'Compagnien' and 'Escadronen' under 'formiren' and 'Mann' and 'Pferde' under 'enthalten'; '—' is the printed dash and the printed fraction 3/4 is written '¾'; leader dots are omitted; the note is given as its own paragraph; other text is omitted. The table's header and its rows above the recapitulation (the 5th column, Bagration's corps and the Guards) are not transcribed. 'Fürst n' is printed so, with a space where the e of 'Fürsten' would be. The rows as read add up to the printed sum in every figure column (114 battalions, 10 companies, 170¾ squadrons, 82575 men and 16565 horses). At the scan's resolution the print's 3 and 8 are hard to tell apart in the men's column and in the raised figures of the fractions; the figures are given as read from the image, and other readings would keep the sum of men (12780 and 9330 for 12730 and 9380, or 6840 and 10340 for 6340 and 10840), so the men of single rows are less certain than their sum. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR.
