# Koch, Mémoires de Masséna, t. V (1850): p.368 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.368 from the registered page image koch-massena-t5-p368-image-v1: the heading of pièce justificative n° VI and the first part of the table 'Ordre de bataille de l'armée d'Italie à l'époque du 18 octobre 1805'. Everything on the page is transcribed, as printed, except the running head and page number. The table is transcribed row by row, as printed, one row per line, with the columns separated by ' | ' (regiment; number of battalions; number of squadrons), and its two-level header as one line. '»' is the printed blank mark and 'id.' is kept. The generals of division and of brigade, printed beside braces, are given on their own lines before their rows, and '[without brigade]' marks a division's rows that no brigade brace covers; the label of a wing or arm, printed beside the brace that covers its divisions ('Aile droite.'), is given on its own line before them. Leader dots, and the period that runs into them after a general's name or 'Total', are omitted. Small capitals are written as capitals, superscript letters inline, and line-end hyphens rejoined. The stray mark printed after 'cheval' in Verdier's artillery row is not transcribed, and in '23e d'infanterie légère' (Verdier's division) the damaged second figure is read as 3. Each division's printed totals of battalions and squadrons equal the sums of its rows. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR.
