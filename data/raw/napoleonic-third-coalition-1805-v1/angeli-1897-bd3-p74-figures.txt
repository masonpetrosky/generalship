# Angeli, Erzherzog Carl als Feldherr, III. Band (1897): p.74 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.74 from the registered page image angeli-1897-bd3-p74-image-v1: the first part of Angeli's table of the k. k. army's positions on 13 October 1805, announced on p. 73 and continued on p. 75. Everything on the page is transcribed, as printed, except the running head, the page number and the scanner's 'Digitized by Google' mark. The table is transcribed row by row, one row per line, with the columns separated by ' | ' (regiment; battalions; squadrons; location) and its header as one line. The headings printed across the table, with the strengths in brackets, are given as their own lines, and the brigade and its commander, printed in the first column beside the rows of the brigade, on their own line before those rows. '»' is the printed ditto mark and '—' the printed dash. A location printed once beside a brace is written on the first row of its group, the other rows of the group being left empty in that column. Leader dots are omitted; '2, Banal-' and 'St Georger-' are as printed. The letter-spaced headings are written as plain text, old-style figures as ordinary digits and the raised-dot hyphen as '-'. The divisions' strengths add up to the wing's (8697 and 16.488 infantry make 25.185, 466 and 2168 cavalry make 2634), and the printed 'Summe' of 44 battalions and 24 squadrons equals the sum of the wing's rows. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR.
