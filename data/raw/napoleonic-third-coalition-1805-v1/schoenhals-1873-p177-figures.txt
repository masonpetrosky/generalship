Manual transcription of printed p.177 from the registered page image schoenhals-1873-p177-image-v1. The table's header and its other rows on this page, from the advance guard to the 2nd column and the 4th column, are not transcribed. In the subtitle, set in a bold condensed type, the dots of the i's are not distinct on the image. 'GL.Przybyczewsky' is printed so, without a space after the period.
