## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.769 from the registered page image alombert-colin-t4-p769-image-v1. The heading of the page's table, its column headings, the rows of the 1st division and of the 1st regiment of hussars with their observations, the row of totals, and the dateline and signature under the table are transcribed, as printed; the other rows are not; words split by a line-end hyphen are rejoined and a hyphen that belongs to a compound is kept ('Unter-Loiben', 'avant-garde'), line breaks are not kept, italics are not marked, superscript letters are written inline ('108e', '4e') and small capitals as capitals ('an XIV'); a colon is written with the space printed before it, a semicolon without one; guillemets are written « and » with the spaces printed inside them, footnote marks are written '(1)' as printed, and footnotes are given after the main text, each as its own paragraph; each table row is written on one line, its cells in the printed order separated by ' | ', and the printed lines within a cell are joined with ' / '; rows that the print brackets under one division label and one observation are written as one row, their cells joined with ' / ' in the printed order; the marks printed in empty cells are written '»', and leader dots are omitted; other text is omitted. The heading and the line 'Le Général chef de l'état-major général,' are in italics. The 1st division's three rows and its observation are bracketed together, and the observation ends with a comma as printed; the label 'Cavalerie.' brackets three rows, of which only the first is transcribed. In the dateline only the upright stroke of the digit before 'brumaire' is printed on the image; it is read as the 4 of the heading. In the row of totals the first digit of the officers' total is indistinct on the image and could be taken for a 1; it is read as 4, because the officers' figures in the rows not transcribed add up to 426, and 426 and 10,244 make the printed total of 10,670 (our arithmetic). Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR.
