# Alombert and Colin, La campagne de 1805 en Allemagne, t. II: p.166 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.166 from the registered page image alombert-colin-t2-p166-image-v1: the return of the cavalry reserve at the Rhine crossing, continued. Every row of the return is transcribed as printed, one row per line, with the columns separated by ' | ': unit; number of battalions (I), squadrons (C) or companies (A and G); men; troop horses; train horses. '»' is the printed blank mark. A label printed beside a brace that groups rows is given on its own line before those rows; '[subtotal]' marks an unlabelled printed subtotal row. Leader dots are omitted, and 'id.' is written with its period. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR. Each printed subtotal and total equals the sum of the rows read above it, using p. 165's printed subtotals, and the printed effectif equals the total present plus those behind and in hospital. The grand park of artillery printed below on the same page is not transcribed.
