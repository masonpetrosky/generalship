## transcription-note

Digital OCR (Tesseract 5.0.0 with the German and Fraktur models, Internet Archive derivative) of a Harvard University copy digitised by Google; not verified against print. The Fraktur OCR misreads many letters (long s as f, 'n' and 'u', 'c' and 'e') and garbles many page numbers. Whitespace collapsed; all other characters retained, including OCR errors, split words, hyphenation marks, page numbers between dashes, signature marks, footnotes and scan stamps interleaved with the text. Local headings are editorial. Bounded passages for the French invasion of the Tyrol by the northern passes and the end of Jellachich's corps in the Vorarlberg, 2-14 November 1805: Moriggl's list of his sources from the preface; Swinburne's report that reached Innsbruck late on 3 November and Archduke John's answer; the written instructions the Archduke left at Innsbruck on the evening of 4 November for holding the Leutasch and Scharnitz and for withdrawing their garrisons; Ney's arrival at Mittenwald, his written summons and Swinburne's answer; the reinforcement of Scharnitz on 3 November; the garrison and the assault on the fortress of 4 November; the taking of the Leutasch work on 4 November, with the protocol of eyewitness statements and a staff officer's judgement he quotes; the morning of 5 November at Seefeld, where Swinburne surrendered, and Ney's entry into the abandoned fortress; and Jellachich's delay, the departure of his cavalry and the capitulation negotiated at Dornbirn. In the Leutasch section the parent repeats the text of pp. 348-349 once, in a second and more garbled OCR, before p. 350; both copies are retained. Page locators are read from the OCR page numbers and are not checked against print unless a figures transcription from the page image is cited. Orders, reports, letters, the protocol and the certificate Moriggl quotes are not separately inspected originals.
