# Alombert and Colin, La campagne de 1805 en Allemagne, t. II: p.162 figures

## transcription-note

Manual transcription of printed p.162 from the registered page image alombert-colin-t2-p162-image-v1: the return of the 5th corps at the Rhine crossing. Every row of the return is transcribed as printed, one row per line, with the columns separated by ' | ': unit; number of battalions (I), squadrons (C) or companies (A and G); men; troop horses; train horses. '»' is the printed blank mark. A label printed beside a brace that groups rows is given on its own line before those rows; '[subtotal]' marks an unlabelled printed subtotal row. Leader dots are omitted, and 'id.' is written with its period. Every figure is read from the image, not from the OCR. Each printed subtotal and the total of men equal the sum of the rows read above them, and the printed effectif of men equals the total present plus those behind and in hospital. The printed effectif of horses (2,728) does not equal 1,861 plus the 267 horses behind (2,128), and '344 bouches à feu' is as printed; both are transcribed as read.
