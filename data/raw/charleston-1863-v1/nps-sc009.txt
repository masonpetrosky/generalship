Charleston Harbor II

413 total (US 413; CS 0;)
Estimated Casualties:
217 total (US 117; CS 100;)
Description:
During the night of September 6-7, Confederate forces evacuated Fort Wagner and Battery Gregg pressured by advancing Federal siegeworks. Federal troops then occupied all of Morris Island. On September 8, a storming party of about 400 marines and sailors attempted to surprise Fort Sumter. The attack was repulsed.
