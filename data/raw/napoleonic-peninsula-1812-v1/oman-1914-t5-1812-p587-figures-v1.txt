## p587-s1
CIUDAD RODRIGO. A. THE FRENCH GARRISON. The garrison, according to Belmas, iv. pp. 282–3, stood on the day of the investment as follows: 34th Léger, one battalion, 975 officers and men effective; 113th Ligne, one battalion, 577; Artillery, 2 companies, 168; Engineers, 15; Non-combatants (Civil officers, &c.), 36; Sick in Hospital, 163; Staff, 3. Total 1,937.
