## p600-s1
It consisted of the 49,636 officers and men accounted for by the return of July 15th printed on the next page, minus some 700 men lost at the combats of Castrillo and Castrejon [also called “combat of the Guarena”] on July 18, and such few hundreds more as may have fallen behind from fatigue during the long marches of July 20–1. Roughly speaking, it must have counted some 48,500 men, as opposed to Wellington’s 50,000.
