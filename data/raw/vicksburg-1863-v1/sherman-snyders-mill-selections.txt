To-day I have felt all the paths aud levees, back, the ground, except the levees, being all under water still; and at 3 p. m. we will open another cannonade to prolong the diversion, and keep it up till after dark, when we shall drop down to Chickasaw, and so on back to camp. I hear the enemy has crossed over to Biggs’ plantation in yawls, doubtless to see what we are about. They will not find out much.
