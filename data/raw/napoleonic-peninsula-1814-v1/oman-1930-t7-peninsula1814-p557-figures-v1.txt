## p557-s1
Artillery: British 1,510; Portuguese 440; 1,950. It is impossible to give the numbers of engineers, train, &c., as the figures in the returns include all those present at the siege of Bayonne and at Bordeaux. The total must have been under 500. Total: Infantry 40,706; Cavalry 6,490; Artillery 1,950; 49,146.
