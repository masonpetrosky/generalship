## p512-s1
This did not arrive till April 26th, when Gazan sent him Soult’s copy of the armistice of the 17th, and notified his adhesion to the new government. Only then did the tricolour flag come down from the Citadel. The garrison still counted 11,800 men under arms—with several thousand sick and wounded.
