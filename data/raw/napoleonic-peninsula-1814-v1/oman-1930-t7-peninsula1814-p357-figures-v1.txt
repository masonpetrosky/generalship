## p357-s1
Deducting the five battalions which were absent from their divisions this day, Wellington had with him in his seven divisions and three cavalry brigades 38,000 bayonets and 3,300 sabres, as also 54 guns and about 1,500 more of other arms, artillery, sappers, &c.
