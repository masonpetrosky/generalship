## Passage and figure treatment

Narrative files retain exact page-bounded half-open ranges from their registered OCR parent, collapsing whitespace only. Short exact OCR quotations anchor the surrounding inspected page context; spelling/OCR defects remain visible. Some initial navigation anchors were paraphrases: these were replaced by actual parent text before sealing, not registered as transcriptions. `selection-audit-v1.json` keeps that alignment history and the final exact ranges/anchors. The minimum navigation similarity is 0.8333; similarity is not an accuracy score. The image controls the English rendering. Numerical figures use separate manual image-transcription sections; no figure is reconstructed from OCR. Typographic/line/table layout normalization is stated in each file.

Available armies, initial garrisons, local engaged troops, reinforcements, sick, officials and terminal survivors remain separate. Horses/formations/losses/prisoners are not converted into opening people totals. Dates of agreement, ratification, command handover, physical departure and occupation are not interchangeable. Frozen outcomes and intervals remain unchanged even where the source wording disagrees.
