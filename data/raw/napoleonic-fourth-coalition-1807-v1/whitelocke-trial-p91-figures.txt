## p91
A. 7822, exclusive of 200 sailors.

A. Solely from captures.

A. I had none.

Q. You have stated that after the arrival of the troops at Miserare, there was a regular supply of provisions, both of bread and cattle; was that in consequence of any previous arrangement, or solely from captures?
A. Solely from captures.
Q. Had you any means of supplying the army with provisions subsequent to their arrival at Miserare, if no provisions had been found there?
A. I had none.
