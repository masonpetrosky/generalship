# Peninsula 1812 combined measurement pass v1

The owner accepted one bounded population/time/authority pass for the complete
eight-entry development group selected by blocker inventory v1. This combines
the work in one table and stops after the campaign. It uses the 24 already cited
non-Bodart strength/command images and four Bodart images. No further source
fetch, separate review, calibration score, outcome fit or feature admission is
authorized by this pass.

## Measurement target and objects

The proposed tactical target remains combat-unit personnel, including officers
and artillery crews, physically available in an explicitly bounded local area
immediately before the first hostile contact, including relevant outposts. Use
the same rule for both sides. A source-listed start date, unopposed investment,
construction, rejected negotiation, main attack or later counterattack cannot
silently replace that boundary. Source-relative phases and reported clocks remain
source-qualified; no invented synchronization or appointment time is supplied.

Each strength observation preserves one source-specific people total and its
population, reported time, precision, derivation and applicability gap. A source
may report a garrison, infantry bayonets, marching army or distributed attack
force; these are not interchangeable. A printed point is not proof of exactness.
An explicit unknown has a null amount and no fabricated supporting citation.

Each authority observation preserves one actor label, role, scope and time.
Compiler labels, garrison government, army direction, local troop direction,
subordinate tasks, succession and post-action orders stay distinct. These are
role appearances, not distinct people or weights for dividing campaign credit.
All global person IDs and army identities stay null unless separately established.

A source-local co-report links an explicit people observation and actor role
within the same source/page/force context. It is useful measurement evidence,
but does not establish complete opening personnel, global identity, continuous
authority or a cross-engagement army. It does not change a blocked side mapping.

The combined records index every original strength/responsibility claim, including
unknowns, without altering the frozen dossier. Bounded atomic observations need
not transcribe every component or every name on a page. The complete original
claims and source pages remain accessible. All eight entries and sixteen sides
remain in the denominator, whatever the mapping result.

## Passage and provenance contract

Every adopted historical proposition cites an inspected passage with source ID,
section/locator, exact quote, English interpretation, raw/metadata/image hashes
and section-specific document date. Existing dossier citations retain their claim
and citation indices. Additional context on these same pages can cite an existing
registered full OCR parent with an exact raw substring and explicit page image.
The image must descend from that parent; a matching phrase alone does not locate
the correct page or verify historical meaning. OCR corruption remains in the
quote; the marked English interpretation follows the inspected printed image.

The source snapshot copies complete existing metadata and its parent/facsimile
closure. No source registry or raw file is changed. Original returns mediated
by Oman/Belmas remain uninspected originals. Reprints and shared material are not
new independent witnesses; the original per-entry three-family ceiling holds.
Pages outside these 28 are not adopted or claimed re-inspected by this pass.

The frozen frame, full siege intervals, old chronology/name alternatives and
dossiers are preserved. In particular, a later French or allied field army is
not added to a local garrison or counted as another campaign success. Nothing
in this table codes morale, causal skill, win probability or campaign contribution.

## Arithmetic and selection

Printed component sums can be checked. Subtracting explicitly listed sick and
non-combatants from Rodrigo's or Badajoz's mixed table is a diagnostic residual
only. Even correct arithmetic does not establish a complete combatant population,
the status of every retained category, persistence to first contact, local
availability or command. No residual or source hierarchy selects an opening
strength. Oman's Salamanca reconstruction remains his inference, not our fitted
or reconstructed opening count.

The sixteen side records list a proposed local area, source-relative boundary
candidates, observed alternatives and specific blockers. A successful source-local
co-report remains separate from an admitted opening pair. Null selected boundary,
strength, person and army fields must not become rows, defaults or imputed values.

## Verification and stopping

The primary inspects every adopted passage on its pinned full page, checks all
claim/side/page counts and arithmetic, runs the offline tests and `make check`,
regenerates with `make reproduce`, and inspects the resulting report. Source and
dossier hashes, the prior inventory, code and actual validation logs are retained
in a versioned primary-verification record. No independent review is represented.

`python3 -m generalship.napoleonic_measurement --write` writes the combined JSON
and Markdown report; the command without `--write` checks them. Shared Makefile
integration leaves earlier frozen modules, tests and verification records intact.

Stop after this complete campaign, with unsupported mappings visible. Do not turn
its unresolved sides into another automatic boundary/person/army follow-up series.
Any deeper source work must identify the particular admission or methodological
decision it could change and have its own bounded scope. A historical model run
still requires a complete design, resolved measurement inputs and owner authorization.
