## Decisions and limits

- **All six opening strengths remain unknown.** Only Cedar Mountain has frozen
  numerical forces, **24,898 (US 8,030; CS 16,868)**. Ropes says Banks's corps
  present did not reach 8,000 and Jackson had 20,000–25,000, while NPS says
  Jackson went to Gordonsville with 14,000 before A. P. Hill joined. The other five
  records have formation labels and live zeros, which are not measured absence.
- **Casualty disagreements remain** (frozen/live): Cedar Mountain **2,707 / 3,900**,
  Rappahannock Station **225 / 0**, Manassas Station **1,100 / 0**, Thoroughfare
  Gap **100 / 0**, Second Manassas **22,180 / 22,179**; Chantilly agrees at 2,100.
  Ropes prints Cedar Mountain's Union loss as 2,393 but its components sum to 2,384.
  His Confederate figures are not bounded to August 28–30: one is an official
  killed-and-wounded list "at Manassas Plains, in August, 1862", the other Jackson's
  total "from the Rappahannock to the Potomac". He says Federal losses can only be
  conjectured without returns.
- **Result labels:** Rappahannock Station and Chantilly are frozen as Inconclusive,
  with Chantilly's "Confederate strategic victory" qualifier; the live pages say
  Indecisive. Second Manassas's retreat is "precipitous" in NPS but "not a rout"
  in Ropes; neither is adopted. Ropes calls the August 28 fight a drawn battle
  inside the frozen August 28–30 interval; it is not a separate engagement.
- **What Pope knew is disputed.** NPS says Pope attacked on August 30 seemingly
  unaware Longstreet was present; Ropes says Pope knew Longstreet had joined but
  thought the reinforcement small and dismissed Porter's information.
- **Command roles:** the order Banks received at Cedar Mountain is disputed in
  Ropes itself; Banks's decision to attack is tagged `commander_created`, as is
  McDowell's unordered detachment of Ricketts to Thoroughfare Gap. Winder's death,
  Kearny's and Stevens's deaths, Stuart's command of the Manassas Junction capture
  and several subordinate commands mean no listed commander receives automatic
  sole credit. Live heading ranks for Longstreet and Lee differ from the frozen ranks
  and are not adopted.
- **VA024 is a frozen aggregate operation** (Bristoe, Kettle Run, Bull Run Bridge,
  Union Mills). Its component figures are not added to its aggregate, and its local
  outcomes differ. The adjoining August 22–25, 25–27 and 28–30 intervals share
  troops; no additive campaign credit is implied.
- Attributed judgments stay attributed, including Ropes's view that Cedar Mountain
  was won by the larger army and NPS's claim that Thoroughfare Gap virtually ensured
  Pope's defeat. Neither is a causal estimate.

The seven null unknowns are six opening strengths and Cedar Mountain logistics.
