## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.**
  - MS012 covers Meridian from the entry on the 14th to the departure on the 20th. The skirmishes on the
    march from Vicksburg, the Yazoo River expedition and the return to Canton are context.
  - MS013 covers February 22. The actions near West Point (20th) and at Ellis' Bridge (21st) are context.
  - Expedition-wide destruction, captures and losses are not assigned to either record.
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** MS012's side bounds are blank; MS013 has a frozen Union bound of
  7,000 (the expedition's full planned strength). Figures recorded, none adopted:
  - NPS: Sherman's main force 20,000; Smith's cavalry 7,000;
  - Sherman's early-February estimate of the enemy: Loring about 7,000, French 3,000 rising to about
    5,000, Forrest 4,000, Lee's cavalry about 4,000;
  - Polk: the column entering Jackson estimated at 20,000 (February 6), then 35,000–40,000 infantry
    (February 9); his own infantry about 6,000, 1,250 and 1,700, "say 9,000"; his orders call the enemy
    three times his force;
  - Smith: 7,000 in all, of which about 2,000 guarded refugees, animals and pack-mules, leaving a little
    less than 5,000 for action; the enemy at West Point 6,000 or 7,000;
  - Forrest: not over 2,500 against twenty-seven regiments estimated at 7,000 (March 8), and 2,500 against
    6,000–7,000 (February 26); his February 14 scouts' estimate was 10,000–12,000.
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - MS012 railroad destruction: Sherman's subordinates reported 60 and 55 miles; Polk says about 50.
  - MS012 result: Sherman "accomplished all I undertook"; Polk calls the campaign unsuccessful because its
    cavalry was kept from a junction. These are campaign judgments, not the tactical result.
  - MS013 delay: NPS says Smith delayed "against orders"; Smith says the late brigade moved under orders not
    his own; Sherman's instructions asked him to reach Meridian by February 10 "as near the date ... as
    possible".
  - MS013 final fight: Smith says a charge at Ivey's farm routed the attackers; Forrest says his men repulsed
    "the grandest cavalry charge I ever witnessed".
  - MS013 casualties: frozen 150 (US 100; CS 50) against live 532 (US 388; CS 144); Smith's 300 or 400 and
    his expedition total of 319; Forrest's 25, 75 and 8–10 for his own side, 162 prisoners, and an estimated
    800 enemy loss. Forrest's compiled return for February 20–22 is garbled; its total row ends in 144.
  - MS012 casualties: the frozen field is Unknown. The live 170 matches Sherman's aggregate for the whole
    expedition, not the record's interval.
- **Command roles and ranks.**
  - Sherman calls Hurlbut and McPherson "the actual commanders of the troops".
  - NPS says Polk "lost his nerve"; this is recorded as NPS's statement and is not adopted.
  - No listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** No works or ground are tagged `inherited`. All claims stay `unresolved` or `post_outcome`.

The two null unknowns are the opening strengths. No morale/readiness score, probability, causal effect or new
commander ranking is introduced. The cohort, both admission proposals and the baseline are unchanged.
