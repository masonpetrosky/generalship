University of California copy (`atlantabyjacobcox09jcoxrich`), with catalog metadata.

- Chapters IV–VI (pp.33–58), including the Dalton and Resaca losses paragraph;
- the close of Chapter VII (pp.63–69);

- Chapter XV (pp.188–210), split at the Dalton paragraph on p.196;
- Chapter XVI to its loss estimate (pp.211–216);

Cox commanded a division of the Twenty-third Corps, and briefly the corps. His history
is an interested Union account that draws on official reports, Sherman's despatches and
memoirs, Johnston's *Narrative*, Hood's *Advance and Retreat* and Dawes's research in the
returns (Appendix A). Ten running heads are misread in OCR: pp.64, 69, 111, 113, 129,
160–162, 176 and 191 (for example "12Q" for 129 and "170" for 176). Citations on those
pages carry an "inferred page" locator.

## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.**
  - GA013 is an aggregate operation. Kolb's Farm (GA014) and Kennesaw (GA015) lie inside
    its interval but are separate frozen records. GA013 cites only monthly and campaign
    loss totals, as context, and does not repeat or add the component figures.
  - Kilpatrick's loss figure (GA021) spans the whole August 18–22 raid.
  - The Dalton block-house surrender (GA020) is kept separate from the Dalton losses.
  - Campaign-wide totals are context and are not assigned to one record. Examples:
    Cox's monthly returns, Johnston's May 5–July 17 losses, Hood's 5,247 and Hardee's
    "considerably exceeded 7,000".
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** The frozen side-specific bounds are blank for all
  sixteen records, and every live "Forces Engaged" field reads zero. The inspected
  figures are on different dates and bases. None is adopted as a matched opening
  strength.
  - Union effective force at the start of the campaign: Cox gives nearly 100,000, by
    army.
  - Confederate figures for May 1–2:
    - Johnston's 40,900 effective infantry and artillery plus about 4,000 cavalry;
    - Cox's 44,000 effectives, which he says understate those present;
    - Appendix A's present for duty, 52,990 (the April 30 table prints 52,992).
  - Later Confederate figures:
    - Appendix A's reconstructed 71,235 at Resaca and 86,475 before the Chattahoochee;
    - Johnston's July 10 return and Hood's July 31 return;
    - Hood's effective 48,750 on July 18;
    - Johnston's 41,000 infantry and artillery plus 10,000 cavalry transferred.
  - Partial one-side figures:
    - Cleburne's 4,683 muskets;
    - Steedman's 1,800 effective, with his opinions of 6,000 and 3,000;
    - Kilpatrick's 4,500, against his 1,100 for Ross and 4,000 for Jackson;
    - the militia at 3,800 (Cox) and about 1,500 (Johnston).
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - New Hope Church:
    - entrenchment: Johnston says his troops were not entrenched, and Cox disputes it;
    - Wheeler's Cassville wagons: seventy per Cox against 250 per Johnston.
  - Dallas: Logan's prisoners' estimate of Hardee's whole command (25,000) against Cox's
    Bate's-division reconnaissance.
  - Kolb's Farm:
    - Hood's note says he was attacked and took works;
    - Cox and NPS say Hood attacked on his own responsibility and was repulsed;
    - Hooker's "three corps" report.
  - Ezra Church:
    - the attacking corps: Hardee's and Lee's per Logan's prisoners, against Lee's and
      Stewart's per Cox and the frozen row;
    - artillery: Logan says he used none, while Cox says Howard massed artillery;
    - cover: Logan's two reports differ.
  - Utoy Creek: Schofield judged the line an outer line not held in strong force; Cox
    says it was strongly held.
  - Peachtree Creek and Atlanta: Hood blames Hardee; Cox rejects the complaint.
  - Jonesborough: Hardee and Hood dispute the fall of Atlanta.
  - Lovejoy's Station: Kilpatrick's "most perfect rout" against the frozen Confederate
    victory and NPS; a brigade of infantry against Cleburne's division.
  - Loss figures differ in almost every record and are kept separate:
    - Rocky Face: Cox calls the Union loss "trifling", while the frozen result note says
      it was high;
    - the live NPS casualty fields differ from the frozen text at New Hope Church,
      Dallas, Pickett's Mill and Ezra Church;
    - the live zeros at Marietta and Dalton II are not measured absence.
- **Observations, not reconciliations.**
  - The frozen Ezra Church US 562 equals Logan's Fifteenth Corps aggregate. The live
    Dallas US 380 is close to Logan's 379. Cleburne's killed and wounded sum to 448, a
    computation here, close to the live Pickett's Mill CS 450. No frozen or live figure's
    basis is established.
- **Command roles and ranks.**
  - The live pages give Johnston as "Lieutenant Colonel" and Kilpatrick as "Hugh".
  - The frozen ranks are kept; the live ranks are not adopted.
  - No listed commander receives automatic sole credit. Subordinates who acted (Hardee
    at Dug Gap, Logan, Granbury, Laiboldt, Minty) are named as attributed actors, not
    rated.
- **Tags.**
  - No works or ground are tagged `inherited`. Some Confederate lines, such as Mud Creek
    and Kennesaw, were prepared in advance, but their timing against each frozen
    interval is not reviewed.
  - All claims stay `unresolved` or `post_outcome`.
- **Unknowns.**
  - The 19 null unknowns are the sixteen opening strengths and three logistics
    dimensions (Kolb's Farm, Peachtree Creek, Utoy Creek), where no supply evidence
    specific to the action was inspected.

No morale/readiness score, probability, causal effect or new commander ranking is
introduced. The cohort, both admission proposals and the baseline are unchanged, with
zero promoted rows.
