## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.** The blockade of Hampton Roads, the May reconnaissances of Aquia Creek, the night
  march from Hampton and Newport News and the pursuit to New Market Bridge are context.
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** Only Big Bethel has frozen bounds (US 3,500; CS 1,200),
  which resemble Magruder's dispatch figures; their basis is not established. Pierce gives only
  partial regimental figures (850 and 650). The Sewell's Point and Aquia Creek records give vessel
  and gun counts, not personnel, and the two sides' gun counts differ. None is adopted.
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - Sewell's Point: Gwynn says the Monticello was driven off; Eagle says he withdrew for want of
    ammunition. The action lasted an hour and a half (Gwynn) or an hour and fifteen minutes
    (Eagle). Frozen losses are 10, live zero; Gwynn reports none of moment, Eagle two slightly
    wounded.
  - Aquia Creek: Ward claims the terminus batteries were silenced; Ruggles says no material
    damage was done. The frozen 10 losses have no basis; each side reports one wounded at most.
    The Army compilation prints Ruggles's first report as "fired some fourteen shot and shell";
    the Navy reprint reads "with twelve".
  - Big Bethel losses: frozen 87 (US 79; CS 8) against live 84 (US 76; CS 8). Magruder's
    estimates of Union losses rise from ten dead and perhaps fifty wounded to 25–30 killed and 150
    wounded. Pierce records 21 casualties from Union troops firing on each other during the
    approach; they must not be added to any Union total.
- **Command roles and ranks.**
  - Sewell's Point: the frozen and live Union commander, Braine, appears in Eagle's report as the
    officer in charge of the pivot gun. Gwynn gave Colquitt command at the battery and returned
    to Norfolk before the May 19 fire.
  - Aquia Creek: Ruggles is frozen as Colonel and live as Brigadier General; he signs as colonel.
    The batteries were commanded by Captain Lynch, who is not listed.
  - Big Bethel: Magruder is frozen as Colonel and live as Major General; he signs as colonel.
    NPS names Pierce as the immediate Union commander.
  - Live ranks are not adopted, and no listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** The Sewell's Point battery was being built during the action, and the passages do
  not date the Big Bethel works. No claim is tagged `inherited`; all stay `unresolved` or
  `post_outcome`.

The three null unknowns are the opening strengths. No morale/readiness score, probability,
causal effect or new commander ranking is introduced. Nothing in this pass changes a model input.
