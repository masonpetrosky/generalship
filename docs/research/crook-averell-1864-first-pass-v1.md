## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.**
  - Context only: the May 10 New River bridge artillery fight, the railroad destruction, the May 12-13
    skirmishes, the retreat to Meadow Bluff, and Averell's approach and his decision against
    Saltville.
  - The rear-guard fight near Dublin on the afternoon of May 9 is described with Cloyd's Mountain.
  - Expedition-wide loss totals are context and are not split between the two records.
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.**
  - Cloyd's Mountain:
    - the frozen force text and battle strength give 10,000, with the side rows blank;
    - NPS gives Crook's three brigades as 6,100;
    - Crook gives his column as 6,155, with Oley's 400 mounted men joining; Pond says about six
      thousand;
    - Crook estimates the enemy at 4,000 to 7,000;
    - McCausland gives three regiments, a battalion and two batteries against twelve regiments and
      ten guns, and says the enemy aggregated 9,000 while "we never had 3,000 men in all";
    - an inspection abstract in Pond gives Smith's brigade 2,100 effective at the start of the
      campaign.
  - Cove Mountain:
    - the frozen force field reads "Brigades" and NPS "a brigade under" Jones;
    - Averell started with 2,079 officers and men;
    - Averell gives the enemy as 4,500 at Saltville and 5,000 "stated by rebel newspapers".

  None is adopted, and the frozen 10,000 is not traced to an inspected passage.
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - Cloyd's Mountain:
    - the stream at the foot of the ridge: waist deep (Crook) against knee-deep (Pond);
    - the Dublin stores: taken (Crook), saved (McCausland) or destroyed after abandonment (Pond);
    - command: Pond names W. E. Jones with Jenkins, and the reports do not;
    - losses:
      - frozen 1,500 against live 1,226 (US 688; CS 538);
      - Crook's 107 killed, 508 wounded and 28 missing, against Pond's 600;
      - Pond's expedition totals for Crook (109 and 513) are wider in scope;
      - McCausland's 600 Union killed and wounded;
      - the McCausland inclosure's totals read 504 and 538, covering the battle "and subsequent
        operations".
  - Cove Mountain:
    - Confederate command: Morgan and Jones (Averell), Morgan (Pond, McCausland) or Jones (NPS, frozen);
    - result: Averell says the enemy retired after dark; Pond says Averell made off eastward;
      McCausland says Averell was defeated; NPS says the Confederates withdrew;
    - losses: Averell's about 114 killed and wounded against Pond's 123, which equals the sum of
      Pond's expedition figures for Averell (17 + 72 + 34); frozen 300 against live zeros.
- **Command roles and ranks.**
  - At Cloyd's Mountain Jenkins was wounded during the battle and McCausland commanded the retreat.
    McCausland says he objected to Jenkins's dispositions.
  - Crook sent the Second Brigade to turn the right and was with the First Brigade in the assault.
  - Frozen and live ranks agree. No listed commander receives automatic credit.
- **Tags.** No ground is tagged `inherited`: the Confederate line at Cloyd's was placed on May 8, and
  the Cove Mountain position is described only by Averell. Claims stay `unresolved` or
  `post_outcome`.
- **Families.**
  - Crook's and Averell's reports are placed in their existing 1864 groups, `crook-valley-1864-reports`
    and `averell-valley-1864-reports`.
  - McCausland is a new author group, `mccausland-cloyds-mountain-reports`.
  - Pond stays in `pond-shenandoah-valley-1883`.
  - Crook's report is cited only for Cloyd's Mountain, and Averell's only for Cove Mountain, so each
    record keeps three families plus the follow-up.

The two null unknowns are the opening strengths. No morale/readiness score, probability, causal effect
or new commander ranking is introduced. The cohort, admission proposals and baseline are unchanged,
with **zero promoted rows**.
