## Decisions and limits

- **Opening strength remains unknown.** The frozen force text is "Divisions (3,000
  total)" with no side split; NPS gives Averell 2,100 and Fitz Lee about 800. Averell
  was directed to take 3,000, detached about 900 and set out with about 2,100; Fitz Lee
  puts the Union force at not less than 3,000 and gives no count of his own. The claim
  is disputed and no figure is adopted.
- **The withdrawal is disputed**: NPS says mid-afternoon after a localized success;
  Averell decided at 5.30 p.m. and recrossed unpressed; Fitz Lee says the Federals were
  driven back and recrossed about 7.30 p.m. under close pursuit.
- **Casualties differ**: frozen **200 total**; live **179 (US 99; CS 80)**. Averell gives
  a Union aggregate of 80 (footnoted to a revised statement) and Fitz Lee a Confederate
  loss of 133, of which 99 killed and wounded. The live split matches those two figures
  with the sides reversed (a computation here); it is not adopted.
- **Clocks conflict**: Fitz Lee dates the attack on his picket to about 5 a.m. and his
  news of the crossing to 7.30 a.m., while Averell has his column reach the ford at
  8 a.m.; neither timetable is adopted.
- **Command roles**: Averell reports two regiments wavering, Duffié charging three
  minutes too soon, and his own decision to withdraw. Fitz Lee credits Stuart, who was
  present but is not a frozen commander, and records Munford's absence. The live heading
  ranks Fitz Lee Major General against the frozen Brigadier General and his signature.
  No listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- Ford defenses, the stone wall and marshy ground are described by both commanders;
  who built the defenses and when is not established, so the terrain claim is
  `unresolved`.

The null unknown is opening strength. No morale/readiness score, probability, causal
