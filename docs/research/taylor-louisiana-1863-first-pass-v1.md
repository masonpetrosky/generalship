## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.** Major's raid to Thibodeaux, the capture of Brashear City and Bayou Boeuf (June 23-24),
  the river batteries of July 3-10, the withdrawal to the Teche and the Morganza operations are
  context. The live campaign label reads "Taylor's Operations in Louisiana West of Mississip".
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** The only frozen bound is LaFourche Crossing's Union 838;
  every other bound is blank. Every figure is recorded with its date and scope, and none is
  adopted:
  - LaFourche Crossing: Stickney's 502 (June 20), 306 arriving that night, 838 present on June 21
    with about 600 engaged, and his estimate of about 600 Confederates in the charge; Irwin's 838
    by organization, Pyron's regiment reported by Major as 206, and about 1,100 after Cahill arrived.
  - Donaldsonville: Green's citizens' report of 300-500 in the fort, his garrison figure of
    "between 500 and 000" (OCR), and his assaulting party of about 800; Irwin's 180 in the fort,
    1,300-1,500 brought to the attack, and his derived 1,811.
  - Kock's Plantation: Green's not over 800 in Major's brigade and not over 750 in his own; Irwin's
    two Union divisions present and two brigades advancing.
  - Stirling's Plantation: NPS's 1,000-man detachment; Irwin's division of about 2,500; no
    Confederate count.
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - LaFourche Crossing: the Confederate strength (600 estimated against 206 reported for one
    regiment); losses (frozen 267 with US 48, live 268 with US 49, Stickney's table 49, his 53 dead
    counted and his inferred 300, and the regimental history's 108 dead and 40 prisoners quoted by
    Irwin).
  - Donaldsonville: the garrison (180 against Green's figures); the date of Mouton's order (Green's
    "of the 26th ultimo"; NPS June 28); the result (frozen Union victory,
    live Indecisive); losses (frozen and live 324 with CS 301, Green's 261, and Irwin's 124
    prisoners counted and 69 dead buried).
  - Kock's Plantation: the distance of the Union retreat (Green four miles, Irwin about a mile, NPS
    six miles to the fort); guns captured (Green three, Irwin two lost); losses (frozen 463 with US
    430, live 498 with US 465, Irwin's 465, and Green's "little less than 1,000" enemy loss against
    his own 33).
  - Stirling's Plantation: losses (frozen 575 with US 454, live 636 with US 515, Green's 462
    prisoners and 121 loss, and Irwin's 16 killed, 45 wounded and 454 prisoners with Green's loss
    printed as 212 though its components sum to 121).
- **Command roles.**
  - Stickney commanded without regard to rank after Sawtell declined; Major Morgan of the 176th New
    York is credited by both accounts.
  - Green advised against storming Fort Butler on June 27 but attacked under Mouton's order;
    Irwin notes criticism of his recklessness. Bullen commanded the garrison.
  - At Kock's Plantation Irwin blames Colonel Morgan's handling; no inspected passage shows Weitzel
    directing the action, although he is a frozen commander.
  - At Stirling's Plantation Green directed the attack; Dana had just relieved Herron.
  - No listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** Fort Butler and its ditch predate the assault, but their state on June 28 is described
  only by the inspected accounts. All claims stay `unresolved` or `post_outcome`.
- **Families.** Irwin stays in `irwin-nineteenth-corps-1892`. Stickney is placed in
  `stickney-la-fourche-crossing-1863-report` and Green in `green-louisiana-1863-reports`; neither
  author had an earlier registry group.

The four null unknowns are the four opening strengths. No morale/readiness score, probability,
causal effect or new commander ranking is introduced. The cohort, both admission proposals and the
baseline are unchanged, with **zero promoted rows**. `python3 -m generalship check` passes, the unit
