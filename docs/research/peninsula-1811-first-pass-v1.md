## Findings and limits

- **Populations and phases.** Olivenza's 6,425-man French infantry/cavalry roster omits unlisted arms and separates horses. Chiclana's French split between Villatte and Victor, Graham's 5,196 expedition contingent and Belmas's much larger expedition estimates are not interchangeable field totals. Sabugal's over-30,000 planned allied attack is not actual engaged strength. Fuentes retains availability on 1 May and the separate Almeida blockade detachment. Albuera's 20,358 British/Portuguese subtotal excludes Spaniards and leaves staff-present cells unknown. No people are manufactured from formations, losses, ships or horses.
- **Sieges, field actions and chronology.** Figueras's capture, 3-May field action and blockade retain separate populations and outcomes. The frozen blockade begins 4 April, before the inspected night of capture (9–10 April); the frame stays unchanged. Toreno's partial reinforcement success qualifies Belmas's complete-rout description. Campo Maior preserves 20-March agreement versus 21-March delivery and excludes the later cavalry encounter. La Bañeza preserves 24/25 August and the uncertain whole-army versus local-division crosswalk. Arroyomolinos preserves 27/28 October. These are alternatives, not repaired dates.
- **Command scope.** Olivenza distinguishes Soult's siege order and Mortier's corps; Gevora distinguishes Soult/Mortier/Girard tasks. Badajoz retains Menacho then Imaz without an invented handover clock. La Bañeza separates Cabrera's local division from Abadia's army and Toreno's reported delegation to Santocildes/Moscoso. Tarragona distinguishes Campoverde, governor Contreras and Sarsfield's marine-line command. Tarifa distinguishes Victor and Leval, plus Skerrett, Copons and Campbell's superior instructions. No automatic credit accrues to everyone named.
- **Valencia sequence.** September expedition strength, November river dispositions, December reinforced French concentration and January prisoners are separate. Suchet's about 33,000 before the crossing is not Valencia's 3-November opening or all engaged at Guadalaviar. The 18,219 prisoners are post-outcome and Toreno disputes their population basis. Shared crossing claims between Guadalaviar and Valencia create no extra campaign contribution.
- **Evidence limits.** Original dated local returns/orders remain uninspected; national/participant narratives are interested and Portuguese evidence is mediated. French own-side local counts remain missing for several entries, including Arroyomolinos in the assigned Belmas overview. Created crossings, deployment and blockade works may be mediators; none is a causal skill estimate. Provenance verification cannot establish historical truth.
