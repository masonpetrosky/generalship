## Question and limits

The unit is Murat's delegated operational authority in the German operations of
1805, not the Emperor's overall military/policy command and not every French
force mentioned in the source campaign. Retaining the superior's authority is
part of this proposed contrast; it does not mean freezing every later imperial
response regardless of the delegate's decisions. Replacement eligibility is
unestablished. The study has no admitted person/army mapping or comparison row.

The source is the pinned `alombert-colin-t2-ocr-v1`, in family
`alombert-colin-1805`: a retrospective French official history printing earlier
correspondence. The parent and its complete metadata remain unchanged. Multiple
letters, officials and volumes in this family are not independent corroboration.
Neither an original archival order book nor new page images were inspected.

Initial reading checked the nomination/question, task order and direct imperial
instructions with immediate related correspondence. One targeted follow-up
checked title/headquarters, the personal-command announcement, Murat's own
qualification and nearby mission orders for general-scope/transfer clarification.
This was a keyword index plus selected blocks, not a complete reading of tome II
or a claim of silence in the wider literature. Three families was the ceiling,
not a quota. No new source, fetch or second family was needed.

The central reconciliation is `m002`/`m003`/`m008`/`m009`. A broad reported title,
an explicit question, specific authority over a contingency affecting several
corps, and a complaint about orders bypassing the general role coexist. Selecting
only one would overstate either unrestricted authority or its absence. Under
[command-unit specification v1](../napoleonic-command-units-v1.md), retain the
scoped observations and leave the broader authority-use query unresolved.

The park instruction is a useful positive time finding. It says what event was
supposed to end a particular task; missing calendar dates do not erase that
relative condition. It does not prove when the task began or ended in practice.
Similarly, `demain, avant midi` in `m013` is a reporting deadline, not an
appointment endpoint or proof the Emperor received the requested information.

in the new passages remain null. Literal OCR headings are retained, including

is not itself a verified chronology. Page images could correct transcription,
but would not by themselves establish an actual transfer or completed task.
