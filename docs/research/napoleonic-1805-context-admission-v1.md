# 1805 posture and fortification admission audit v1

**Primary-verified proposed-use audit, begun 2026-10-20. No features admitted or historical model fitted.**

## Decision

**No-go for preparing the unchanged joint posture-plus-fortification prediction experiment.** The realized-offensive-role variable describes action during the engagement. It cannot be an unchanged pre-contact condition. This is a variable/use mismatch, not a finding that prior intentions cannot be researched. A planned-role variable would need its own definition and evidence audit.

All 22 frozen entries are retained: 44 engagement-field candidates from 51 source-specific claims. There are **0 complete joint rows**. Source alternatives and repeated citations never add engagements.

The six fortification candidates are Neresheim (`none_named`), Verona (`both_fortified`), Caldiero (`B_fortified`), Scharnitz (`B_fortified`), Austerlitz (`A_fortified`) and Steken (`none_named`). These are source-qualified named-work categories. Neither a positive category nor `none_named` establishes physical absence for an unmentioned side. No two zero indicators are emitted.

The six candidates do not establish a viable modelling sample, a causal fortification effect or a corpus-wide coverage rate. Any fortification-only design must define reporting/missingness, source and coding sensitivities, coextensive force/target rows and grouped evaluation before a separately authorized experiment. The compiler-strength benchmark totals are not silently promoted to opening strengths. **Stop this complete-group audit here.**
