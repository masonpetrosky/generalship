## Distinctions retained

- Roanoke's landing count, table forces, casualties and captured prisoners are
  different populations. Shaw's field surrender is distinguished from Wise's
  principal-commander listing; gunboat support is not erased by army attribution.
- New Berne's frozen **1,080** casualties differ from live NPS **1,085**. Wet marching
  and sailors hauling guns are attributed conditions, not numerical readiness.
- Fort Macon's NPS narrative dates opening fire to **April 26**; Woodbury says
  **April 25**. His surrender-day OCR reads **23th**, left unrepaired. The frozen
  **490** and live **442** casualties remain unresolved. Woodbury's killed/wounded
  counts in the retained context are not automatically comparable to those totals.
- South Mills remains **Inconclusive** in the frozen model despite Woodbury's
  favorable characterization. NPS names Dismal Swamp Canal locks; Woodbury names
  the Albemarle and Chesapeake Canal and a reconnaissance/obstruction purpose.
  These accounts are not silently combined. His later naval obstruction is not
  proof that Reno destroyed locks during the April 19 expedition. Frozen **150**
  casualties and live **139** remain distinct from Woodbury's Union-loss account.
- Tranter's Creek has a formation description and a 25-person gun crew, not a
  reliable total strength. Its frozen **40** casualties and live **zero** remain
  visible. A return to Washington without pursuit is not converted into a missed
  opportunity score. Supply and contemporary information remain unknown.

No listed commander automatically receives all or equal credit. No morale scores,
causal effects or win probabilities are manufactured. Later occupation, blockade
consequences and operations are not extra independent victories.
