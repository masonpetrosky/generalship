## Inspection and stopping record

Read all three frozen battle, force and commander row sets and the three retained NPS
summaries in full. NPS/CWSAC and the Arnold tables are one family. Each battle uses two
further families:

- **Duke**, *History of Morgan's Cavalry* (1867), newly pinned from a New York Public
  Library scan with catalog metadata. Seven Chapter XIV passages are used: the plan of the
  raid (OCR pp.407–411), the starting strength (p.415), Garnettsville (pp.428–430), the
  crossing and Corydon (pp.431–435), Buffington (pp.446–454), the last days and surrender
  (pp.454–458) and the raid's effects (pp.458–461). Duke commanded a brigade on the raid by
  his own account and was captured at Buffington. He is therefore an interested
  participant, and a secondhand one for the days after July 19 and for Morgan's conference
  with Bragg, which he says he did not attend.
- **One Union report per battle**, each a new selection from the already pinned *Official
  Records* Series I, Volume XXIII, Part 1, with its own author group:
  - **Burnside's** July 22 dispatch and the compiled Union casualty return, for Corydon;
  - **Hobson's** report (the day of the month is blank in OCR), for Buffington Island;
  - **Shackelford's** August 1 report, for Salineville.

Dispatches quoted inside a report belong to that report and do not count as separate
families. For example, Kautz's July 18 dispatch is quoted in Hobson's report. Two Duke
running heads are unclear in OCR:

- p.454 reads "45-1".
- p.455 reads "456", so citations on that page carry an inferred locator.

No maps, Indiana Legion, Judah, Way or Rue reports, prisoner rolls or print pages were
inspected. Stop after this batch.

## Decisions and limits

- **Raid-wide figures are not assigned.** These include:
  - the starting strength (NPS about 2,450; Duke 2,460 effective);
  - Burnside's 3,000 on entering Indiana;
  - the Union return for July 2–26;
  - NPS's claim of 6,000 paroled;
  - Duke's campaign judgments.

  None of these is added to any single record. Duke's view that the raid delayed the fall
  of East Tennessee is recorded as his judgment, not as a causal estimate.
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** All three records have imported structured bounds,
  which are unchanged. Reported figures differ in time and scope:
  - Buffington: Duke's "scarcely nineteen hundred" against the frozen 1,700.
  - Salineville: Duke's 250 after Way's fight, Shackelford's "about 400" and the frozen 400.
- **Burnside's return and Corydon.** The return names no Indiana militia unit. Its OCR total
  row ends in 74, which equals the sum of the row's other figures (a computation here). It
  therefore does not account for the frozen 360 Union casualties at Corydon.
- **Disputes preserved:**
  - Corydon: casualties, frozen 401 (CS 41) against live 411 (CS 51).
  - Buffington: the escape count. NPS says about 400 escaped; Duke says 1,100–1,200
    retreated with Morgan and about 300 crossed.
  - Buffington: casualties. Hobson gives 5 or 6 Union and 57 Confederate killed; Duke gives
    about 700 prisoners and twenty-odd killed on each side.
  - Buffington: command on the field. Hobson protested Judah's assumption of command.
  - Salineville: the validity of Morgan's surrender to the militia captain Burbeck. Duke
    records the terms; Shackelford calls them unfair and illegal and refused to recognize
    them.
- **Salineville scope.** Major Way's morning fight and the afternoon surrender near New
  Lisbon both fall on July 26. Which of them the frozen record covers is not established.
- **Command roles.** Judgments are attributed to their authors. The live heading ranks
  Jordan Colonel against the frozen Brevet Colonel; the live rank is not adopted, and no
  listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** Corydon's rail barricades were built within the operation, so they are not
  tagged `inherited`. Terrain, logistics and command claims stay `unresolved`.

The three null unknowns are the opening strengths. No morale/readiness score,
probability, causal effect or new commander ranking is introduced. The cohort and both
admission proposals are unchanged, with **zero promoted rows**. The baseline still uses
**23/127 engagements in 13 eligible groups**, with strength Brier **0.276882** against
**0.250000** for equal odds.
