## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.** The live NPS page gives Liberty Gap as another name, but Cist treats Liberty
  Gap as a separate action of McCook's corps; its 231 Union killed and wounded (Johnson's
  command including Carlin's brigade) are not added.
- **Opening strength remains unknown.** Cist gives the "effective total present" Bragg
  reported for his army at Shelbyville on June 20 as 43,089, and sizes the attack on
  Wilder at two brigades. Bate says fewer than 700, about half his brigade, fought on June 24, later
  giving 650 engaged, and calls the Union force vastly superior. None is adopted.
- **Casualties**: frozen Unknown against live 583 (US 583; CS 0). Bate reports 146 killed
  and wounded of 650 engaged.
- **Command roles**: Bate says he commanded his detachment in person until Stewart arrived
  at sundown; NPS says Stewart ordered the withdrawal on the 26th. No listed commander
  receives automatic sole credit.
- Terrain, logistics and command claims stay `unresolved`.
