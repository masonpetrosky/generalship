## Distinctions retained

- New Madrid/Island No. 10 spans several operations. The canal's **light transports**
  and the ironclads running the batteries are different movements. Mahan's reported
  **7,000 surrendered men** cannot replace missing opening strengths or casualties.
- The frozen narrative dates Mackall's surrender of Island No. 10 to **April 8**;
  Mahan's sequence places an island/garrison surrender to the navy on the evening
  of **April 7**. Date and surrender scope remain unresolved; the frozen interval
  is unchanged. McCown's replacement by Mackall has no established effective clock.
- Memphis's **five gunboats, two initial participating rams and eight opposing
  vessels** are vessel populations, not personnel counts. Mahan says Switzerland
  joined after the fight was decided; the seven purchased rams are not all initial
  participants. Live NPS personnel zeros do not establish absent crews.
- Mahan describes Ellet operating independently within Davis's command area.
  Retain distributed army/naval roles without adopting the author's criticism or
  assigning equal or sole credit to every named commander. His collision narrative
  and surprise claims are not independently adjudicated.
- Memphis's frozen and live **181 casualties (US 1; CS 180)** lack inspected
  category definitions and cutoffs. Ellet's later death is not added to that total.
  Naval result, municipal surrender and later occupation are distinct events, not
  additional independent modeled wins.
