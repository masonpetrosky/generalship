## Inspection and stopping record

Read both frozen battle, force and commander row sets and both retained NPS pages in full.
NPS/CWSAC and the Arnold tables are one family. Each record uses two further families:

- **Britton, *The Civil War on the Border*, volume I** (Putnam's, second edition, revised, 1891;
  Brigham Young University scan `civilwaronborder00brit`). No Scribner "Campaigns of the Civil War"
  volume covers these actions; Britton is the comparable 19th-century history. The title page says
  the work is based upon the official reports of named Federal and Confederate commanders (Blunt,
  Herron, Hindman, Marmaduke and Shelby among them); the preface says he served with the Federal
  army there and almost invariably took the official reports as his guide for numbers and
  casualties. It is therefore not independent of the report families and is treated as partly a
  participant's interested Union account. Selected: the title page and preface; Chapter XXIX, Action
  at Cane Hill (OCR pp.385–395); Chapter XXX, Battle of Prairie Grove (pp.396–429). The volume's
  parent is the registered `britton-border-1-ocr-v1` (catalog `ia-britton-border-1-metadata-v1`).
  This pass drafted byte-identical copies as `britton-civil-war-border-1-ocr-v1` and

## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.** Cane Hill is one record from the first contact near Cane Hill through the Cove
  Creek defile; Jewell's November 23 reconnaissance and the December 4–6 outpost skirmishes are
  context. At Prairie Grove, Herron's forced march and the December 8 truce are context.
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** Both frozen records have blank numerical bounds and the
  live Forces Engaged fields read zero. Every figure is recorded with its basis and none is
  adopted:
  - Cane Hill: Britton's seven thousand for Marmaduke (Blunt's information on the 24th) and five
    thousand men with thirty guns for Blunt's march on the 27th; Shelby's brigade list and his
    "at least twelve guns" on his position. Britton names three Confederate brigades against the
    frozen two.
  - Prairie Grove: Hindman's 9,000 infantry, 2,000 cavalry and 22 guns on December 3; his estimates
    of Blunt (7,000–8,000, then 3,000–4,000 more) and Herron (4,000–6,000 infantry, 2,000 cavalry,
    30 guns); his "less than 10,000" left for the fight; Britton's "upwards of twenty thousand"
    (Blunt's information), twenty (eighteen elsewhere) and twenty-two guns, Blunt's three thousand
    cavalry on arrival and three thousand not brought into action.
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - Cane Hill result: the frozen and live Confederate tactical victory against Britton's account
    of successive Confederate withdrawals and a truce.
  - Cane Hill losses: frozen 475 (CS 435) against live 85 (CS 45); Britton relays Blunt's 4
    killed and 36 wounded and 75 Confederates killed or mortally wounded; Shelby gives none.
  - Prairie Grove result: Hindman's claim to hold the field against his withdrawal that night.
  - Prairie Grove losses: Hindman's 350 (December 9) against his 164 killed, 817 wounded and 336
    missing (December 25); his Federal loss claims; Britton's Union 167, 798 and 183 (1,148 as
    printed components) against the frozen US 1,251.
- **Command roles and ranks.**
  - The live pages list Marmaduke without a rank and style Hindman a Brigadier General (frozen
    Major General); the live Prairie Grove page omits Blunt. Live ranks are not adopted.
  - At Cane Hill, Britton puts Blunt in personal direction of the pursuit and the Cove Creek
    charge; Shelby says Marmaduke ordered him back. At Prairie Grove, Hindman says Shoup chose
    the ridge position; Britton says Herron decided to attack and Blunt commanded all three
    divisions after arriving.
  - No listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** No ground is tagged `inherited`; the positions were taken during the actions. All
  claims stay `unresolved` or `post_outcome`.

The two null unknowns are the opening strengths. No morale/readiness score, probability,
causal effect or new commander ranking is introduced. No model input, cohort file, admission
proposal or baseline is changed.
