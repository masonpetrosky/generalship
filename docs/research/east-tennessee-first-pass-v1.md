## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.** The following fall outside the frozen dates and are not added:
  - at Blountsville, the September 19 Bristol raid, the September 20 skirmish near
    Zollicoffer and the October 13–14 fighting;
  - at Blue Springs, the October 3 and 5 skirmishes and the October 11 fighting at
    Henderson's and Rheatown.
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** Blountsville has an imported Confederate bound
  (1,200) and a blank Union bound; Blue Springs has none. Williams's own estimates of the
  force against him rose from "at least 5,000" to "not far short of 15,000". Burnside's
  6,000 is the Ninth Corps' strength on arrival. None is adopted.
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - Blountsville: who set the town on fire. Foster says the Confederate shells did;
    Jones says the Federals did.
  - Blountsville: the timing. The frozen description has an attack at noon; Foster says
    he met the enemy at 9 a.m. and charged just before dark.
  - Blountsville casualties: Foster's about 6 killed and 14 wounded against the frozen
    Union 27.
  - Blue Springs: the evening assault. Burnside says it drove the Confederates from their
    first line; Williams says it was repulsed with heavy loss.
  - Blue Springs: Williams's purpose. The frozen description says he meant to take Bull's
    Gap; Williams says he was making a diversion for Ransom.
- **Casualties.** Burnside's "about 100" includes the pursuit, beyond the frozen Blue
  Springs date.
- **Command roles and ranks.** Judgments are attributed to their authors. The live heading
  gives "Colonel John Williams" against the frozen Brigadier General; the live rank is not
  adopted, and no listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** Terrain, logistics and command claims stay `unresolved`; no `inherited` tag
  is used.

The two null unknowns are the opening strengths. No morale/readiness score, probability,
causal effect or new commander ranking is introduced. The cohort and both admission
proposals are unchanged, with **zero promoted rows**. The baseline still uses **23/127
engagements in 13 eligible groups**, with strength Brier **0.276882** against
