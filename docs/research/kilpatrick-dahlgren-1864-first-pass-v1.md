## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.** The record is read as the night ambush of Dahlgren's separated detachment near Walkerton
  on March 2, which its name and date identify. The frozen description and commander rows also cover
  the raid: Ely's Ford, the March 1 attacks before Richmond, Hampton's night attack near Atlee's and
  the pursuit toward Old Church. Those are context; raid-wide strengths and losses are not assigned
  to this record.
- **Opening strength remains unknown.** The frozen force field reads "Brigades" and the live page
  gives zeros. Every figure is recorded with its date and scope, and none is adopted:
  - raid: 4,000 ordered (Pleasonton; also Humphreys and NPS); "in all 3,582 strong" at the start
    (Kilpatrick's report); 3,585 at the start and 3,317 on March 6 (his March 6 dispatch);
  - Dahlgren's advance: 460 (Kilpatrick) or 500 (Humphreys, NPS);
  - the ambushed party: about 100 (Kilpatrick's report and March 6 dispatch, NPS) or about 80 with a
    large number of negroes (his March 8 dispatch); Captain Mitchell rejoined Kilpatrick with upward
    of 300 of Dahlgren's party (Kilpatrick's report; 300 in Humphreys);
  - the ambush force: Pollard's own company of about 25, McGruder's about 30 with a number of home
    guards, and Fox's about 15. Kilpatrick speaks of a large force of citizens and regular soldiers.
    Fox's own count (read, not selected) is not used.
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - Time of the ambush: about 11.30 p.m. (Pollard); Wednesday night (Humphreys); "Thursday evening,
    11 p. m." (Kilpatrick's March 8 dispatch), which the OR compiler footnotes as Wednesday, March 2.
  - Confederate command: Pollard says he chose and manned the position and that Fox took command on
    arriving; Stuart's indorsement credits Pollard's dispositions; Humphreys credits Fox and Pollard
    chiefly.
  - Losses:
    - Dahlgren killed and 7 wounded (Kilpatrick, March 8); Dahlgren and several wounded (Pollard);
    - about 175 captured, 40 of them negroes (Pollard), against all but 21 taken (Kilpatrick);
    - frozen "Unknown" against live 100 (US 100; CS 0);
    - raid-wide, less than 150 lost (Kilpatrick, March 3).
  - The Dahlgren papers. NPS and Lee's letter (printed by Humphreys) describe orders to destroy
    Richmond and kill Davis and his cabinet. Meade and Kilpatrick deny authorizing them; Kilpatrick
    says the approved address lacked those passages. Fitzhugh Lee says the papers were not altered.
    The dispute is recorded, not resolved.
- **Command roles and ranks.**
  - The frozen commanders (Hampton, Kilpatrick, Dahlgren) reflect the raid. No inspected passage
    places Hampton or Kilpatrick at Walkerton.
  - Dahlgren was killed at the first fire, and Kilpatrick says Major Cooke then commanded the party.
  - The live page lists Kilpatrick as "Hugh" and Hampton as Lieutenant General, and omits Dahlgren;
    the live rank is not adopted.
  - No listed commander receives automatic credit.
- **Tags.** No ground is tagged `inherited`: the ambush position was chosen during the pursuit. The
  papers claim is `post_outcome`; the rest stay `unresolved` or `post_outcome`.
- **Families.**
  - Kilpatrick's reports are placed in his existing group, `kilpatrick-atlanta-reports`. The older
    Carolinas v1 record's group `kilpatrick-savannah-report` is the same author, and same-author
    groups are not independent corroboration.
  - Pollard is a new author group, `pollard-walkerton-1864-reports`.
  - Humphreys stays in `humphreys-gettysburg-rapidan-1883`.

The null unknown is the opening strength. No morale/readiness score, probability, causal effect or new
