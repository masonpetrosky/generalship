  later march fall outside the record.
- **Opening strength remains unknown.** Each commander assigns the larger number to the
  other side. None of these figures is adopted:
  - Averell's infantry, 1,175;
  - Averell's prisoner-based estimate of the Confederates, about 4,000;
  - Echols's own force engaged, about 1,700;
  - Echols's estimate of the Union force, about 7,000;
  - Echols's figure for the two Union columns, nearly 10,000.
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - The retreat: Averell calls it a total rout; Echols describes some confusion in an
    otherwise controlled withdrawal.
  - Casualties: the frozen 526 against the live 415 (US 140; CS 275). Echols's 275 equals
    the live Confederate figure; Averell reported about 100 of his own and about 250
    Confederate killed and wounded.
- **Command roles.** Averell credits Moor; Echols assumed command and ordered the
  withdrawal. These are attributed judgments, and no listed commander receives automatic
  sole credit.
- **Tags.** When the breastworks were built is not established. Terrain, logistics and
