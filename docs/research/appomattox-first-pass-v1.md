## Decisions and limits

- **Scope and overlaps.**
  - Dinwiddie Court House (VA086) and White Oak Road (VA087) are both March 31.
  - The April 2 assault (VA089) and Sutherland's Station (VA090) are the same day.
  - Rice's Station (VA092), Sailor's Creek (VA093) and the first day of High Bridge (VA095)
    are April 6. VA095 joins Read's April 6 raid and the Second Corps' April 7 crossing, and
    Cumberland Church (VA094) is the afternoon of April 7.
  - VA093 includes the Second Corps' fight with Gordon at Perkinson's mill, since the frozen
    other names include Lockett Farm and the frozen commanders include Gordon.
  - The Deep Creek fight of April 3 is not assumed part of Namozine Church (VA124).

  Totals that span more than one record are not assigned to one record:
  - Humphreys' 571 for the Second Corps on April 7 (High Bridge and Farmville);
  - the Adjutant-General's Five Forks prisoner entries, which include Dinwiddie, Hatcher's Run
    and April 2;
  - Sheridan's and Gibbon's campaign casualty tables;
  - Humphreys' Union loss of 9,944 for March 29 to April 9;
  - the army-wide returns (cited in VA085; VA086 also cites the same paragraph's 13,000 present
    for duty of Sheridan's cavalry; nothing is summed across records).
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** The frozen side-specific bounds exist only for VA086
  (US 45,247; CS 20,030), with no stated date or population. The other thirteen records carry
  formation words ("Corps", "Divisions", "Brigades", "Armies") and blank bounds, and every live
  force field reads zero. Every figure found is recorded with its date, basis and scope, and
  none is adopted, among them:
  - The spring 1865 effective forces from the latest returns: Army of the Potomac infantry
    69,000; 124,700 of all arms in the three Union commands; Lee not less than 46,000 infantry
    and 57,000 of all arms.
  - Appendix L: the Army of the Potomac's 3,064 officers and 68,956 enlisted infantry present
    for duty equipped on March 31; Lee not less than 55,500 on the February 20 return. Humphreys'
    57,000 and 55,500 differ in basis and are not reconciled.
  - February 20 return strengths assigned to formations:
    - the two Lees' cavalry, 5,760;
    - Pickett's and Johnson's infantry at Five Forks, 6,600;
    - Wilcox's four brigades, about 4,000;
    - Gordon's corps, about 7,600.
  - Sheridan's 9,000 effective cavalry, as assembled when Crook's division reported on March 27
    (the figure is not itself dated); Mackenzie's about 1,000; the present for
    duty of Sheridan's enlisted cavalry, 13,000.
  - Sailor's Creek: Humphreys' reconstruction of Ewell's 3,600 and Anderson's 6,300, from
    Kershaw's 2,000, Custis Lee's about 1,000, Johnson's about 3,800 and Pickett's about 2,500.
    Ewell's "about 3,000 each" at the evacuation, and the 30,000 he was told engaged against him.
  - High Bridge: Read's 500 infantry and 80 cavalry.
  - Farmville: Humphreys' "probably about 18,000 infantry" for Lee's whole army.
  - April 9: Lee's 7,892 armed infantry, 63 guns and not more than 2,100 cavalry; Fitzhugh
    Lee's 2,400 mounted (via Humphreys); the unattributed 8,000 armed.
