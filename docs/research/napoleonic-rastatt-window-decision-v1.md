English interpretation: the Emperor intends Murat to move his headquarters to
Rastatt and remain until the park has passed that position; he also requires
personal observation of its passage near Kehl and a report. The preceding part
of `m010`, lines 35209–35214, directs Murat to tell Baraguey-d'Hilliers to protect
the park if needed and supply a guard battalion, placed at the park director's
disposal during the movement. These are prescribed responsibilities, not evidence
that any act occurred. A battalion designation supplies no people total here.

The Emperor's superior authority and the order's task constraints are retained

## Window alternatives and disposition

The [command-unit specification](../napoleonic-command-units-v1.md#4-authority-and-time-rules)
allows source-relative windows and explicit analytical conventions. A complete
biography, synchronized clock, global identity registry and strict first-contact
strength pair are not prerequisites for retaining this local task. The question
is whether the proposed comparison has the support its own use requires.

| Proposed window | Methodological assessment | Current disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Document heading to park passage at Rastatt, covering all protection/observation/reporting | Issuance is not effective receipt. The endpoint explicitly qualifies headquarters residence; extending it to all duties would add an unsupported scope assumption. | Reject as an evidenced whole-mission interval. The corrupt OCR heading cannot supply a normalized t0. |
| Actual headquarters arrival at Rastatt to park passage there, residence duty only | A narrower task is coherent, but beginning only upon successful arrival would omit delay or failure to move headquarters from the replacement comparison. Such a window could describe residence after arrival, not the whole assigned duty. | No observed start/end or coverage is established in this record. Do not select it as the replacement window. |
| Order becomes operative for the delegate to the specified residence-ending event | A possible **design convention**, including preparation and compliance from task assignment, rather than conditioning on later arrival. Receipt/operative authority and event coverage would need source support or defensible, explicit assumptions. | Not selected: no operative anchor, observed completion/closure, or coverage basis is supplied here. Changing a field label cannot supply those observations. |
| Document heading to planned movement dates, or an arbitrary fixed duration | A calendar interval can be an analytical convention, but planned movement is not execution. A chosen duration still needs a defended relationship to task authority and observed data. | No such interval is justified by the current case. Do not manufacture one to obtain a usable row. |

An event-based endpoint is **not inherently invalid**. The
[methods design](../napoleonic-methods-v1.md#1-what-campaign-contribution-would-mean)
requires an endpoint rule fixed without selecting on the commander's realized
success. The residence condition is stated in the order, so it can inform a
prospective rule. It does not establish that the event happened.

If a later design used first passage as an endpoint, it would need to define
the rule in each replacement scenario, including delay, non-passage, a superseding
order and incomplete observation. It cannot hold the incumbent's observed passage
time fixed for every replacement while claiming to measure changes in elapsed
time. Completion and duration would be outcomes, not covariates used to erase
the contribution being assessed. A fixed follow-up horizon or another explicit
closure rule might be defensible in a future design; neither is chosen here.
Unknown completion cannot become success, failure, a zero duration or exclusion
of an inconvenient opportunity.

## Preparation and the counterfactual boundary

A late task-specific t0 would define a different contribution from the full
appointment. Earlier work by the same person, if it preceded that t0, would be
inherited for this narrow contrast; it must not then be described as the person's
total campaign contribution. Within-window organization, coordination and supply
may be consequences of the replacement and must not all be controlled away as
inherited state. The present record does not assign actual preparation, resources
or information to either side of a selected boundary.

Retaining imperial authority does not freeze every later imperial decision or
every subordinate response. Those reactions and shared responsibilities require
an interference/identification argument. No marshal list or rank supplies an
eligible replacement pool. Those further requirements remain unresolved, but
the **window gate already fails**; they are not excuses to begin resource or
outcome extraction on an unsupported interval. No causal effect follows from
obedience, park passage or a later predictive improvement alone.

Uncollected resource and outcome values are not, by themselves, a reason to
reject a window; those can be collected after a defensible design is chosen.
Here the unresolved elements are the operative assignment anchor and the match
between the duty being compared and its endpoint. A symbolic residence-only
design is possible, but it is not an evidenced interval for the broader mission.
This no-go is a bounded research-allocation decision on the current record,
not a claim that every relative-event design needs completed outcomes first.
