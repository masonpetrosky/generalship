## Decisions and limits

- **Scope and overlaps.** No frozen record overlaps another. Campaign-wide figures are context and
  are not assigned to one record:
  - Kilpatrick's campaign losses (1 officer killed, 6 wounded and 2 missing; 31 men killed, 153
    wounded and 166 missing);
  - his November 14 strength of 5,500;
  - Wheeler's "never exceeded 3,500".

  Actions outside the frozen dates are context only:
  - the Griswoldville skirmishes of November 20–21;
  - Wheeler's night attack of November 26–27;
  - the Rocky Creek and Thomas' Station skirmishes;
  - the Tullifinny operations.
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** Three records have blank frozen bounds (GA025, GA026 and
  GA027). Every figure is recorded with its date, basis and scope, and none is adopted:
  - **Griswoldville.** Woods counts 1,513 present for duty and estimates about 5,000 attackers.
    Smith says his 600 losses were over a fourth of his effective muskets; his division had 1,900
    muskets on leaving Lovejoy's, plus about 400 State Line. He says the First Brigade of militia
    was not engaged and that Cook's Athens and Augusta battalions were; the engaged total is
    unknown.
  - **Kilpatrick's division.** 5,500, in brigades of 2,800 and 2,700, on November 14.
  - **Buck Head Creek.** Wheeler had only about 1,200 across the creek.
  - **Honey Hill.** Hatch's about 5,500 of all arms against Smith's about 1,400 effective muskets
    (frozen 5,000 and 1,400).
  - **Fort McAllister.** Hazen's division had an effective strength of 4,426 on November 15, and
    nine regiments assaulted. The garrison is 120 (frozen), about 200 (Cox) or 250 including
    killed (Hazen).
- **Disputes preserved** (13 claims marked `disputed`: five force-scope, five casualty and three
  result):
  - **Buck Head Creek's result.** The frozen field reads Union victory and the live field
    Indecisive. Kilpatrick reports a repulse at all points; Wheeler reports routing Kilpatrick in the morning
    and a charge beyond the creek that ended at a line cavalry could not approach.
  - **Waynesborough.** Kilpatrick reports a complete rout; his account of December 4 names only
    units of his own division. Wheeler says infantry advanced and he withdrew with difficulty.
  - **Honey Hill's withdrawal.** Hatch reports an orderly retreat without pursuit; Smith calls it
    a flight.
  - **Casualty figures:**
    - Griswoldville: Woods gives 94 against the frozen US 62;
    - Honey Hill: Smith estimates Union losses at upward of 1,000 against Hatch's 746;
    - Fort McAllister: the garrison loss is 71 (frozen), 250 (live), 48 (Cox), or 250 captured
      including killed (Hazen);
    - Waynesborough: Wheeler cites 50 killed and 147 wounded "according to his own account".
- **Command roles and ranks.**
  - The frozen row lists John Hatch as Major General; he signs as Brigadier-General.
  - The live pages list "Hugh Kilpatrick" and omit Wheeler at Griswoldville.
  - Cox names the Fort McAllister commander "Major G. W. Anderson" against the frozen George A.
    Anderson.
  - Woods relays prisoners' reports that "Major-General" Phillips was killed.
  - None of these is adopted.
  - Smith says Colcock held the immediate executive command of the main line under his direction.
  - No listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** Two terrain claims are tagged `inherited`: the Honey Hill redoubt, "said to have been
  built two years since", and the Fort McAllister works. Works built during the actions stay
  `unresolved`. Outcomes are `post_outcome`.
- **OCR limits.** Page locators rely on OCR running heads:
  - Kilpatrick's pp.362 and 363 are flagged as misread;
  - Hazen's p.111 is flagged as reading 'Ill';
  - Wheeler's first page is taken as 406 by inference, but no cited passage falls on it.

  Several quotes preserve OCR corruption, for example "74G", "3o0" and "Onr loss".

The five null unknowns are the five opening strengths.

No morale/readiness score, probability, causal effect or commander ranking is introduced. The v1
cohort, the frozen baseline and both admission proposals are unchanged, with zero promoted rows.
