## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.** The Grafton advance, Pegram's July 13 surrender, Garnett's retreat and Corrick's
  Ford, Floyd's Gauley crossing, the pursuit after Carnifex Ferry, Lee's Valley Mountain advance
  and the Elk Water demonstrations are context.
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.** All seven frozen bounds are blank and the live fields
  read zero. Recorded, not adopted:
  - Philippi: Morris's company counts; Nicolay's "about two regiments" and "something over a
    thousand" for Porterfield.
  - Rich Mountain: Nicolay's 1,900 in Rosecrans's column against Pegram's 3,000; 310 at Hart's
    with one gun (Pegram) or two (Nicolay); Pegram's 1,300 in all and no more than 350 engaged.
  - Cross-Lanes: five regiments and three guns, or 5,000–10,000, relayed from Cox; Wise's itemized
    2,600 for Floyd.
  - Carnifex Ferry: Floyd's under 2,000 (and 1,800) against 8,000–9,000; Rosecrans's five
    regiments and probably sixteen guns.
  - Cheat Mountain: Reynolds's 9,000, 5,000 and about 300 engaged; Rust's 1,500–1,600 and the
    prisoners' 4,000–5,000.
  - Greenbrier River: Reynolds's about 5,000 against his estimate of 9,000; Jackson's 5,000, then
    6,000–7,000.
  - Camp Alleghany: Jones's 700 against 2,500; Johnson's 1,200 effective against 5,000.
- **Disputes preserved.** Every record's losses differ between frozen, live and report figures,
  for example:
  - Rich Mountain: frozen 346 against live 162; Rosecrans (via Nicolay) 12 killed and 49
    wounded; Pegram's own 40–45 killed.
  - Carnifex Ferry: Rosecrans's 20/100 and 16/100 against Floyd's 20 wounded.
  - Camp Alleghany: Johnson's 144 against the frozen Confederate 146.

  Results:
  - The frozen and live pages agree at Philippi, Rich Mountain, Cross-Lanes, Carnifex Ferry and
    Cheat Mountain, but the reports dispute the duration at Rich Mountain (two hours against
    three) and the outcome at Carnifex Ferry (Floyd claims five repulses).
  - Greenbrier River and Camp Alleghany are frozen Inconclusive; each side claims success.
  - At Greenbrier River the Union called the movement an armed reconnaissance, and Jackson an
    attempt to storm his camp.
- **Command roles and ranks.**
  - Live ranks differ from frozen: Tyler (Brigadier General live, Colonel frozen), Rosecrans
    (Major General live), Lee (Major General live, General frozen). They are not adopted.
  - Morris is frozen and live as Colonel; the Official Records style him a brigadier general of
    Indiana militia.
  - Garnett is listed for Rich Mountain but held Laurel Hill; no inspected passage places him in
    the action.
  - Wise's account of Cross-Lanes is that of Floyd's co-commander, written after his recall; he
    was not present.
  - No listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** Floyd built his Carnifex breastwork, Jackson's Camp Bartow works were unfinished, and
  Johnson's works had been suspended. No claim is tagged `inherited`; all stay `unresolved` or
  `post_outcome`.

The seven null unknowns are the opening strengths. No morale/readiness score, probability,
causal effect or new commander ranking is introduced. Nothing in this pass changes a model input.
