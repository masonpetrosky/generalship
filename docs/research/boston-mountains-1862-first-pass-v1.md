## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.**
  - Kirksville's frozen interval runs to August 9. The later dispersal of Porter's command is
    known here only from McNeil's secondhand mention and NPS.
  - Cooper's December 15 report narrates the October 4 evacuation of Newtonia, which is outside
    both records.
  - Campaign-wide movements are context.
- **Opening strengths remain unknown.**
  - Frozen numeric bounds exist for Independence (US 344, CS 700), Lone Jack (US 800), Newtonia
    (US 1,500) and Clark's Mill (US 113, CS 1,000).
  - Two do not match their own text and equal the frozen Union casualty figure. At Independence the
    text reads "approx. 300" and 344 is the casualty figure. At Clark's Mill the text reads
    "approx. 100 men" and 113 is the casualty figure.
  - Independence's CS minimum and maximum are both 700 against a text of 700–800.
  - The inspected figures:
    - Kirksville (McNeil): 1,000 aggregate and about 500 engaged, against 2,500 to 3,000.
    - Independence: Buel's 312 effectives and his officers' 700 to 800 attackers; Britton's 400 to
      500 present for duty.
    - Lone Jack: Foster's 800 ordered and 740 on the 16th against about 3,000 and Coffee's 1,500;
      Britton's 806 and not less than 3,000.
    - Newtonia: Salomon's 150-man scouting party and prisoners' 7,000; Cooper's 4,000 against 6,000
      to 7,000.
    - Old Fort Wayne: Blunt's 4,000 to 7,000 or near 7,000, and less than 600 with the Second
      Kansas; Cooper's 1,500 against at least 5,000.
    - Clark's Mill: Barstow's 110 against 1,750 or not less than 1,000; Wickersham's 100 against
      1,000.

  None is adopted.
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - Independence:
    - wounded: Buel's 30 against Britton's 74;
    - direction of the Confederate withdrawal: Kansas City (NPS) against Blue Springs (Britton);
    - who commanded at the surrender (Thompson or Hays; Britton is internally inconsistent);
    - the reported collusion charge against Buel and Rodewald's defence.
  - Lone Jack:
    - Confederate ammunition: Cockrell's statement to Foster against Britton;
    - Foster's claim to have "whipped" the enemy against the frozen Confederate victory;
    - Britton's casualty components, which sum to 254 (a computation) against frozen US 160 and
      live US 272.
  - Newtonia: frozen CS 100 against live CS 78, neither found in the inspected passages.
  - Old Fort Wayne:
    - the Texas regiments, joined the day before per Blunt's informant, detached per Cooper;
    - the result: Cooper's "skirmish" judgement;
    - losses: Blunt 4 killed and about 15 wounded (dispatch), then 1 killed and 9 wounded; Cooper 6
      killed and about 30 wounded. The frozen CS 150 equals the figure Blunt attributes to
      Confederate wounded; that is an observation, not a reconciliation.
  - Clark's Mill:
    - the commander ("General Green" per Wickersham; Burbridge with Greene second per Barstow);
    - losses: Wickersham's 2 killed and 2 wounded match Barstow's morning skirmish, not his totals
      of 7 killed and 2 wounded. The live CS 34 equals Barstow's enemy killed.
  - Kirksville: McNeil's two loss reports (5 killed and 25 wounded, then 32 wounded) do not match
    the frozen US 88. The execution of 15 paroled prisoners is kept separate from battle losses.
- **Command roles and ranks.**
  - The live pages style McNeil and Cooper Brigadier General; the frozen ranks are kept.
  - At Old Fort Wayne, Cooper says he was ill and Buster commanded. At Newtonia, NPS names Blunt's
    army, but Blunt is not a listed commander and does not appear on the field in the inspected
    passages.
  - Brawner, Coffee, Warren, Hall, Buster and Watie are attributed actors,
    not rated.
  - No listed commander receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** All claims are `unresolved` or `post_outcome`. Stone fences and village buildings
  predate the actions, but their use was improvised; nothing is tagged `inherited`.

The six null unknowns are the opening strengths. No morale/readiness score, probability, causal
effect or new commander ranking is introduced. The cohort, admission proposals and baseline are
unchanged, with zero promoted rows.
