## Findings and limits

- **Troop populations.** Rodrigo's 1,937-person mediated investment table includes sick and non-combatants. Badajoz's 5,000/5,003 garrison observations differ from 12,000 investing bayonets and 45,000/51,000 wider army estimates. At the June Salamanca siege, the local force of Clinton's division is separate from the whole army on San Cristobal heights; no people count is manufactured from the division. Castalla retains Spanish 12,000 distributed across sectors versus Suchet/Bodart's 10,000 advancing population. Burgos's wider 28,000 Anglo-Portuguese and 11,000 Galicians are not all local besiegers.
- **Arithmetic and definitions.** Rodrigo's component sum is 1,937; Badajoz's infantry and all-category sums are 3,861 and 5,003. July Salamanca's allied table totals 51,939 by both national subtotals and officer/men columns. That explicitly includes officers and the Spanish contingent; the definition and timing crosswalk to Bodart's 46,000 infantry/cavalry reference remains unresolved. Oman's French about 48,500 is explicitly his reconstruction from an earlier return and losses/stragglers, with train/combatant ambiguity; it is not a new own-side opening count. Arithmetic checks do not prove historical accuracy.
- **Authority.** Altafulla retains Belmas's Lacy attribution beside Eroles in Toreno/Bodart. Badajoz separates Wellington's army command and Beresford's initial investing corps. Castalla distinguishes Harispe's reserve from Delort's local action and Mesclop at Ibi. Marmont's account reports his disabling wounds and Bonnet–Clausel succession, without an invented handover time or acceptance of his blame narrative. Burgos distinguishes Dubreton's fortress command from the field relief army.
- **Chronology and crosswalks.** Peñíscola's 4-February possession is retained beside Bodart's 2-February endpoint; Badajoz's 6–7-April storm precedes its frozen 9-April endpoint. The June Salamanca label “Fort San Cristobal und San Vicente” stays frozen alongside accounts identifying San Cristobal as the army's field ridge and Saint-Vincent, San-Gayetano and La Merced as the three siege forts. Surrender on 27 June is distinct from July's battle. Burgos keeps 21-October orders and night 21–22 withdrawal. No frame correction or additive siege/field/relief campaign credit follows.
- **Unresolved evidence.** Dated matched local returns and original orders remain uninspected; own-side people counts are missing for several sides. Portuguese/Spanish returns through Oman and French tables through Belmas/Oman remain mediated. Secret preparation, intercepted dispatches, sketches, deployment and siege means are source-specific observations, not morale/readiness scores or causal skill estimates. No automatic credit is assigned to all named commanders.
