## Decisions and limits

- **Scope.** The October 1 skirmishes at Clinch Mountain and Laurel Creek Gap, Gillem's and Ammen's operations
  in East Tennessee and the withdrawal to Kentucky are context.
- **Killing of wounded prisoners.** The claim `shooting-of-wounded-prisoners` (outcome, `post_outcome`)
  records two statements:
  - NPS: Confederate soldiers "were said to have murdered, captured, and wounded black soldiers";
  - Gardner: armed men he believed to be Confederate soldiers shot 5 wounded Black privates at the field
    hospital on October 3, and 2 wounded Black prisoners at Emory and Henry College Hospital on October 7;
    men in Confederate uniform shot Lieutenant E. C. Smith there on October 8; and he was told of further
    outrages after he left.
  No Confederate account of these events was inspected, so the claim records statements and does not settle
  identity or number. These deaths are not added to the battle's casualties. Brisbin's report on the Fifth
  U.S. Colored Cavalry, which bears on the same question, was read and not selected.
- **Opening strength remains unknown.** The frozen force field reads "Divisions (11,000 total)", with a
  battle-level strength of 11,000 and no side bounds; the live field reads zero. Figures recorded, none
  adopted:
  - Burbridge: 2,500 engaged; from prisoners, the enemy 6,000–8,000 (read "G,0()0"), with Breckinridge said
    to be present with 4,000; on October 10, 6,000–10,000 (the first figure read "0,000");
  - Jackson: the enemy variously estimated at 4,000–8,000, with 2,000 dismounted reported on the Russell
    road; 300 reinforcements arrived "in a state of perfect insubordination".
- **Disputes preserved.**
  - Result: the frozen result is Confederate victory; the live Results field reads "Union Victory" and is
    not adopted. Burbridge claims to have whipped the enemy at every point before withdrawing for want of
    ammunition.
  - Date: NPS dates the attack to the morning of October 1; the frozen date and Burbridge give October 2.
    Jackson's dispatch printed as October 2, 5 p.m., still expects the attack; its printed date is kept but
    not reconciled.
  - Casualties: frozen 458 total and live 458 (US 350; CS 108). Burbridge's 350 matches the live Union figure;
    the Confederate 108 has no inspected basis.
- **Command roles and ranks.** Burbridge and Jackson are Brigadier Generals in the frozen and live records.
  Burbridge names Echols, Williams, Vaughn and possibly Breckinridge against him. Who commanded the
  Confederate defense during the action is not settled by the inspected passages. No listed commander
  receives automatic sole credit.
- **Tags.** No claim is tagged `inherited`; the approach gaps and the works were contested during the
  operation. All claims stay `unresolved` or `post_outcome`.

The null unknown is the opening strength. No morale/readiness score, probability, causal effect or new
commander ranking is introduced. The cohort, both admission proposals and the baseline are unchanged.
